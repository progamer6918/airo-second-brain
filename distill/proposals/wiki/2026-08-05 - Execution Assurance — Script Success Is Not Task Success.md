## 💡 Pelajaran
A successful script or shell command execution (EXIT_CODE=0 / SCRIPT_SUCCESS) does not prove a task complete. Required evidence must be satisfied explicitly. When required evidence is missing, ASB fails closed (BELUM_TERBUKTI / CAN_ADVANCE=NO).

## 🤔 Kenapa Layak Diingat?
Mencegah klaim kelulusan palsu (false PASS) di mana alur eksekusi sukses tetapi hasil validasi bisnis/governance sebenarnya belum terbukti.
