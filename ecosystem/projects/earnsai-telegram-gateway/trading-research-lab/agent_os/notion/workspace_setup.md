# EarnsAI Notion Setup

Use only the dedicated EarnsAI/AI Notion account and workspace.

Required root page:
- EarnsAI Command Center

Required databases:
- Research Journal
- Strategy Registry
- Backtest Logs
- Decision Journal
- Risk Flags
- Agent Activity Log

Do not connect personal Notion workspace.
Do not paste NOTION_TOKEN or database IDs into chat.
Keep NOTION_WRITE_MODE=DRY_RUN_ONLY until guarded write is approved.
