# Interesting Facts

- Prompt
    
    Generate 50 sets of engaging and polarizing facts in the style of the examples provided. Format them into a table with three columns:
    Topic, Part 1, and Part 2. Ensure there are no repetitions. The topics should include interesting fact you didn’t know, amazing facts about the world, funny and interesting facts, interesting facts in real life, interesting and mysterious facts, and more. Each set should consist of a topic, followed by a Part 1 and a Part 2. The language complexity should be suitable for a reading level under grade 7. Here's a few examples:
    
    Topic: Interesting Facts You Didn't Know
    
    Part 1: When rain falls, it's not actually tear-shaped as commonly drawn. Raindrops are actually shaped like hamburger buns as they fall through the air.
    
    Part 2: Currently, more people in the world own mobile phones than toothbrushes. There are about 6.8 billion mobile phone users, but only 4.2 billion people own toothbrushes.
    
    Topic: Amazing Facts About The World
    
    Part 1: There's a town in Canada that has special bunkers for bears. In Churchill, Manitoba, residents deliberately leave their car doors unlocked so people can take shelter if they encounter polar bears.
    
    Part 2: In Norway, you can see the sun for 24 hours straight during summer. This phenomenon is called the "Midnight Sun" and occurs because of the country's position near the North Pole.
    
    Topic: Funny and Interesting Facts
    
    Part 1: Wombats are the only animals in the world that produce cube-shaped poop. Scientists only discovered why in 2021 - it happens because their intestines have both elastic and rigid sections.
    
    Part 2: Flamingos can't eat unless their heads are upside down. They have to flip their heads to filter food from water with their unique beaks.
    
    Topic: Interesting Facts in Real Life
    
    Part 1: When you cry due to emotions, your tears have a different chemical composition than when you cry from cutting onions. Emotional tears contain stress hormones.
    
    Part 2: The human big toe provides 40% of the foot's pushing power when walking. Without our big toes, we would have great difficulty maintaining balance.
    
    Topic: Interesting and Mysterious Facts
    
    Part 1: There's a song that can cure headaches. Research shows that the song "Weightless" by Marconi Union can reduce anxiety by up to 65% and help relieve headaches.
    
    Part 2: Even after hundreds of years, scientists still can't fully explain why we yawn and why yawning is contagious. Even reading the word "yawn" can make someone yawn!
