# Commercial Program Intelligence

## 🍼 10 detik

Program komersial bukan angka diskon yang berlaku selamanya. Program adalah **paket time-bound** dengan periode, tipe, channel penjualan, support, syarat, eksekusi, dan mekanisme klaim.

## Default WorkDesk rule

Kalau diagnosis mengarah ke commercial competitiveness:

`tentukan periode kasus → resolve program yang berlaku pada periode itu → pilih revision yang applicable → cek tipe/channel → bandingkan package → cek execution/claim constraints → baru simpulkan impact`

## Yang harus dipisahkan

- formal Juklak vs quick sales reference;
- current vs historical;
- cash vs credit applicability;
- consumer offer vs internal contribution;
- price-list number vs actual DP/discount paid;
- program eligibility vs claim validity;
- program source rule vs WorkDesk recommendation.

## Source authority model

For commercial-program facts, prefer the formal applicable program document / official source. Quick-reference MSW/PL visuals are convenience layers and may not replace formal eligibility/claim rules.

## Private memory dependency

Exact current amounts, program IDs, claim dates, and internal evidence requirements live in the private WorkDesk sidecar and must not be published in public ASB by default.

## Diagnostic use

Commercial program becomes a root-cause candidate only after localization and evidence testing. A competitor or Honda program existing in the market is not sufficient; WorkDesk still tests **change, contrast, magnitude, and mechanism**.
