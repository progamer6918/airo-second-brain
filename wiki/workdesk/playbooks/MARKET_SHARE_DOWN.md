# Playbook — Market Share Turun

## 🍼 Mulai dari sini

Jangan langsung bikin promo. Market share turun berarti **share Honda turun terhadap total market pada scope yang sama**; penyebabnya harus dibuktikan.

## Route

1. Validate denominator, period, geography and segment.
2. Compare Honda volume vs total market vs competitor movement.
3. Break down segment/type/series contribution.
4. Break down kabupaten/area/dealer contribution.
5. Check stock/availability and pricing/fincoy competitiveness.
6. Check people/productivity and sales funnel.
7. Check activity frequency/effectiveness and coverage.
8. Check dealer execution/system/NOS only where relevant.
9. State root cause in a way that explains the measured gap.
10. Build quantified corrective action + owner/PIC/timing/control metric.

## Evidence basis

`WD-SRC-020` PICA Market Share; `WD-SRC-005` advanced PICA/marketing sense; `WD-SRC-006` Owner penetration project.
