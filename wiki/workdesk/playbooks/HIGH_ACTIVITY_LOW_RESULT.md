# Playbook — Activity Banyak, Hasil Kecil

## Core question

Apakah problem-nya **quality/frequency/targeting activity**, atau activity-nya benar tetapi funnel/execution setelahnya bocor?

## Check

`activity → traffic/leads → contact/follow-up → prospect → SPK/approval → delivery`

Then inspect:

- target customer/area fit;
- activity frequency and execution quality;
- lead capture completeness;
- assignment/follow-up speed;
- sales-person productivity/skill;
- financing/approval/reject/cancel;
- stock/availability;
- offer/pricing;
- competitor counter-activity.

## Rule

Do not report “activity ineffective” without a conversion/coverage or outcome comparison.

## Evidence basis

`WD-SRC-004` activity formulation; `WD-SRC-005` marketing-sense frequency/effectiveness; `WD-SRC-058` CRM/leads; Owner recurring work context.
