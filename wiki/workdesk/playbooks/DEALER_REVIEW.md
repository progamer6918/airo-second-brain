# Playbook — Dealer Review

1. Define review period and target gap.
2. Market issues: local positive/negative drivers, competitor, customer/economic signal.
3. Dealer performance: sales forecast/actual, share, stock, cash-credit/fincoy.
4. People: count, productivity, target/achievement, capability.
5. Funnel/activity: source, frequency, conversion, follow-up.
6. Network execution: channel/coverage/NOS/system as relevant.
7. Isolate root cause; do not list symptoms as causes.
8. Build PICA with quantified target, PIC, timing and evidence.
9. Review progress routinely; Dealer Review is a diary, not visit decoration.
