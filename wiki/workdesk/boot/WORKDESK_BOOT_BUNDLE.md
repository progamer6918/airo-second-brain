# AIRO WorkDesk

AIRO WorkDesk is the professional-work domain inside AIRO Second Brain (ASB).

Its job is not to store filenames. Its job is to transfer usable professional understanding across humans and AI consumers with evidence, provenance, source authority and progressive UX.

## Main Objective

Compile the supplied professional corpus into a validated, provenance-preserving, human-friendly and AI-portable knowledge system so a zero-context reader can understand and work without reconstructing old chats.

## Initial Domain

Area Sales Supervisor work inside the Honda Main Dealer ecosystem, including market/area, dealer/network, sales planning, ASSDP, NOS, ND Plan/Reshape, customer/CRM, leadership, data/analytics, presentation, and Owner applied projects.

## Product Shape

1. Human cockpit in Obsidian.
2. Semantic knowledge in `wiki/workdesk/`.
3. AI boot and task router.
4. Evidence manifests and atomic claim ledger.
5. Practical playbooks.
6. Comprehension and navigation tests.

- Full source digestion / visual review / row-level spreadsheet validation: not yet complete.

# WorkDesk Source Authority & Conflict Rules

Authority is contextual.

## Current operational-rule question

1. live/current verified evidence;
2. current official/current-year source;
3. Owner-confirmed current business rule;
4. formal training not superseded;
5. historical official reference;
6. presenter notes;
7. AI-cleaned summaries;
8. public research;
9. chat-derived discovery;
10. model memory.

Example: current NOS => 2026 checklist beats NOS 2023.

## Theory / taught-framework question

Use the relevant formal curriculum as primary evidence: ASSDP for Honda functional framework, BWI for taught leadership/communication, external expert material for its own management concepts.

## Owner working-pattern question

Use current work evidence → current Owner instruction → Owner applied projects → distilled historical work context → model memory.

## Notion/presenter notes

Supplementary only. Label `CORROBORATED`, `PLAUSIBLE_CONTEXT`, `TRANSCRIPTION_RISK`, `AI_DERIVED_SUMMARY`, `OWNER_APPLIED_NOTE`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, or `SECRET_EXCLUDED`.

## Evidence mechanics

Important knowledge should be traceable through `evidence/workdesk/CLAIM_LEDGER.tsv` and source IDs in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.tsv`.

Known conflicts/uncertainties are tracked in `evidence/workdesk/CONFLICT_REGISTER.tsv`.

## Public safety

Owner-approved distilled business/training knowledge may be public. Never commit passwords/tokens/auth artifacts, raw private transcripts, or unnecessary sensitive personal/customer data.

# Area Sales Supervisor — Operating Model

## 🍼 10 detik

Area Sales Supervisor (ASS) bukan sekadar mengawasi sales. ASS mengelola **business performance area**: memahami market, menyusun target/forecast, membaca dealer, menggerakkan people/network, mengontrol execution, lalu memperbaiki hasil.

## Operating cycle

### 1. Understand
Market, economic drivers, customer, competitor, area, dealer, sales/stock/fincoy.

### 2. Plan
Forecast market, target, demand, distribution, people/productivity, activity and network development.

### 3. Execute
Coordinate with AHM, dealer, financial company and cross-function; ensure strategy/activity runs.

### 4. Check
Monitor daily/weekly/monthly performance, dealer review, market share, sales/stock, economic/competitor movement.

### 5. Act / Improve
Build PICA/corrective action, propose strategy/activity/program, develop network/people and control sustainability.

## Advanced role evidence

Advanced SPV-role material adds a standardized dealer-review lens and expects deeper activity formulation:

- target & achievement by type/people;
- stock days;
- cash/credit and fincoy approval/reject/cancel;
- hyperlocal market information and forecast;
- customer/migration indicators;
- historical trend and area mapping;
- sales-force count/productivity;
- activity frequency and target;
- county/dealer/stock/fincoy/series/daily-sales monitoring.

## Default diagnosis discipline

`result gap → exact scope → market/segment/area → dealer/channel → commercial drivers → people/productivity → activity/funnel → execution/system/NOS → root cause → quantified action → control`

Not every branch is mandatory. Evidence determines the path.

## Evidence

- `WD-SRC-026`, slides 4–7 — ASS role expressed through PDCA.
- `WD-SRC-004`, slides 4–9 — Advanced SPV role/dealer review/activity formulation/process monitoring.
- `WD-SRC-005`, slides 6–25 — advanced wrap-up across market research, forecast, targeting, sales/stock and PICA.
- `WD-SRC-006`, pages 3–19 — Owner Advanced project applying DMAIC to market penetration.
