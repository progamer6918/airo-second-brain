# ASSDP Capability Ladder

## 🍼 10 detik

ASSDP menunjukkan perkembangan dari **mengontrol area → menyelesaikan business problem → membentuk strategy dan memimpin execution**.

## Basic — control the operating system

Evidence families cover channel management, PDCA, target breakdown, dealer review, forecasting, demand, sales/stock, pricing/financing, PICA/KPI, MyHero, INS/NOS/TTM, plus interpersonal/presentation/goal/RCA/change.

Working question: **Can I understand and control the operating basics of my area?**

## Intermediate — solve the business problem

Evidence covers negotiation, problem solving, business case, dealer review, H1 case, CRM/HC3 workshop and Owner AT High strategy.

Working question: **Can I diagnose, build a business case and align stakeholders around an intervention?**

## Advanced — shape and lead the business

Evidence covers SPV role, market research/forecast/marketing sense, penetration, deeper monitoring, influence/coaching/creative thinking, customer/business model/organizational change, and Owner penetration project using DMAIC.

Working question: **Can I find opportunity, shape strategy, mobilize the network and sustain the result?**

## Important

Advanced does not invalidate Basic. Current/newer material wins only where it explicitly updates or conflicts with older material.
