# Coaching & People Development

## Why coaching belongs in WorkDesk

A supervisor cannot solve every dealer problem by personally supplying the answer. Coaching converts diagnosis into capability building.

## Core competencies

1. Being Present.
2. Active Listening.
3. Questioning.

## GROW

- Goal - desired result / priority.
- Reality - current situation, evidence, obstacles, attempts.
- Options - alternatives/resources/support.
- Will - exact commitment and timing.

## Session operating flow

### Opening
- prepare with prior data/context;
- build relationship;
- clarify confidentiality;
- define duration;
- set agenda and desired session outcome.

### Digging
- use open questions;
- listen for key words;
- move from story to reality/gap/options;
- stimulate, do not hijack the thinking.

### Closing
- move from exploration to action;
- record 1-3 actions;
- confirm commitment;
- define follow-up/next session.

## Coaching vs directing

Use direct instruction when:
- safety/compliance is non-negotiable;
- the person lacks minimum information;
- urgent correction cannot wait.

Use coaching when:
- the person has enough context to think;
- ownership/capability matters;
- the problem requires reflection and choice.

Advanced supervision needs both.
