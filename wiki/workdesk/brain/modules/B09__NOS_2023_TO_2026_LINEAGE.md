# NOS 2023 -> NOS 2026 Lineage

## 2023 foundation
The Regular H123 workbook already operationalized:
`H1/H23 x Premises/People/Process -> audit result -> mandatory items -> consolidated result.`

It used Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum grading and a Result Summary across six H1/H23 components.

## 2026 evolution
Batch04 shows that the current supplied NOS system is more differentiated by network type and more digitally integrated. The 2026 corpus separates Regular H123, Regular H1, AHASS H23, POS, BTL and other network treatments, and adds Reshape/digital/customer-app/EV/loyalty operating context.

## Resolver rule
- Historical question about 2023 standard -> use 2023 workbook/deck.
- Current NOS audit question -> use applicable 2026 network-type workbook.
- Never combine old and new checklist items silently.
