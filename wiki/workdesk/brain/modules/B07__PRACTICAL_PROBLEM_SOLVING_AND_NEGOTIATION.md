# Practical Problem Solving + Negotiation

## Two different jobs

Problem solving determines **what should be changed**. Negotiation/influence helps secure **agreement/resources/behavior needed to execute the change**.

Do not mix them:
- A weak diagnosis cannot be fixed by better persuasion.
- A correct diagnosis can still fail if stakeholders do not agree to act.

## PPS flow

`actual-vs-desired gap -> breakdown/Pareto -> facts/data -> hypotheses -> test -> 5 Whys -> root cause -> temporary + real solution -> PIC/due/status`.

## Negotiation preparation

Before asking for dealer/partner commitment define:
- BATNA;
- reservation value;
- aspiration;
- likely ZOPA.

Then select an influence approach supported by the training source: social proof, liking, contrast, time-machine/future visualization, higher purpose.

## Application to dealer supervision

Example:
- Diagnosis says a dealer needs additional weekend activity and dedicated follow-up capacity.
- PPS establishes why and how much.
- Negotiation prepares what minimum commitment is acceptable, what alternative exists if the dealer refuses, and how to frame the business benefit.

The result should be a negotiated operational commitment, not a motivational speech detached from evidence.
