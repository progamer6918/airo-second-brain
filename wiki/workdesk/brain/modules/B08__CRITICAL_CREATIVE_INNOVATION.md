# Critical + Creative Innovation

## Ambidextrous thinking

The source pairs:
- **critical** -> exploitation / improvement;
- **creative** -> exploration / new possibilities.

Innovation needs both.

Creative without critical screening produces exciting but fragile ideas. Critical without creative exploration optimizes the old system forever.

## Improvement side

Continuous improvement uses PDCA and standardization:
`Plan -> Do -> Check -> Act -> standardize -> improve again`.

## Creative side

Questions:
- WHAT IF?
- “Bagaimana kalau?”

SCAMPER:
- Substitute;
- Combine;
- Adopt/adapt;
- Modify/Magnify;
- Put to other use;
- Eliminate;
- Rearrange/Reverse.

Creative association should borrow mechanisms from other contexts/industries, then adapt rather than copy blindly.

## Critical side

Ask:
- Is the pattern consistent?
- What is the evidence?
- What are the implications?
- Is it rational/realistic?
- What are the facts?
- Why?

Idea selection must explicitly consider applicability and risk.

## Innovation loop

`Problem/opportunity -> expand possibilities -> screen evidence/risk -> prototype/test -> measure -> standardize or discard`.
