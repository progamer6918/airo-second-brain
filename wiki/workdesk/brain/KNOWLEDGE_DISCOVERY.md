# Cari Tahu di WorkDesk

Pilih **hal yang ingin dipahami**, bukan folder atau nama training.

## Pasar, area, customer, competitor
[[domains/01_BUSINESS_SENSING_MARKET_INTELLIGENCE|Business Sensing & Market Intelligence]]  
Pakai saat ingin memahami market size/share, denominator, economic/local drivers, area/ring/coverage, customer, competitor, atau kenapa pertumbuhan volume tidak sama dengan pertumbuhan share.

## Target, forecast, sales, stock, distribution
[[domains/02_TARGET_FORECAST_DEMAND_SUPPLY|Target, Forecast, Demand & Supply]]  
Pakai untuk target deployment, forecasting, Niguri, sales/stock hierarchy, stock days, distribution, product/series mix, dan feasibility.

## Dealer, territory, productivity
[[domains/03_DEALER_TERRITORY_PERFORMANCE|Dealer & Territory Performance]]  
Pakai untuk Dealer Review, TTM, channel management, target ownership, activity, salesperson productivity, dealer diagnosis, dan area performance.

## NOS, network, AHASS, POS, Reshape, BEST
[[domains/04_NETWORK_DEVELOPMENT_OPERATING_STANDARD|Network Development & Operational Standard]]  
Pakai untuk current NOS 2026 logic, tier differences, mandatory gating, INS, NetDev, Reshape, customer-journey standard, dan monthly operating control.

## CRM, leads, BTL, VE, customer experience
[[domains/05_CUSTOMER_CRM_ACTIVATION|Customer, CRM & Activation]]  
Pakai untuk CDB quality, lead funnel, conversion, BTL/roadshow/exhibition/showroom event, Virtual Exhibition/Showroom, customer needs/journey/loyalty.

## Problem solving, strategy, project, business case
[[domains/06_PROBLEM_SOLVING_STRATEGY_BUSINESS_CASE|Problem Solving, Strategy & Business Case]]  
Pakai untuk PDCA, PICA/PPS, DMAIC, Design Thinking, hypothesis testing, root cause, breakthrough strategy, project governance, dan business case.

## People, coaching, influence, change, presentation
[[domains/07_LEADERSHIP_PEOPLE_STAKEHOLDER|Leadership, People & Stakeholder Execution]]  
Pakai untuk SPV role, coaching/GROW, negotiation/BATNA/ZOPA, influence, presentation, meeting, critical/creative thinking, change, resilience, time/focus.

## Kasus nyata & pola pembelajaran
[[domains/08_CASE_MEMORY|Case Memory]]  
Pakai untuk membandingkan kasus baru dengan historical Owner/external cases tanpa mengubah hasil kasus lama menjadi causal forecast.

## Kalau bingung mulai dari mana

## Advanced / evidence
Untuk formula, authority/currentness, ambiguity, provenance atau fidelity: buka `reference/`. Untuk source-level fallback: `source-memory/`.
