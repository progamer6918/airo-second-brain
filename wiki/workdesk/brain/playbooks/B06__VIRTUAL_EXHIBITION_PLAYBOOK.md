# Virtual Exhibition - Playbook

## 10 seconds

VE bukan soal "berapa leads". Baca funnel lengkap:

`source event -> unique leads -> contacted -> prospect -> deal -> verified retail contribution`.

## Review cepat

### 1. Source benar?
Pisahkan Independent / FINCOY / Product Launch. Jangan campur source lalu bandingkan performanya seolah sama.

### 2. Lead benar-benar unik?
Repeated clicks/customer action bisa membuat raw records ganda. Deduplicate sesuai identity rule/source process.

### 3. Follow-up jalan?
Lihat assignment, contact quality, pending, re-follow-up, stock/color/indent.

### 4. Deal terverifikasi?
Latest supplied 2025 process says deal should flow through NMS/SPK/SO and from Mar-2025 is counted by chassis number, not phone number.

### 5. Customer journey ada friction?
Survey 2025 menyorot navigation/ease dan slow response. Jangan hanya menambah traffic kalau bottleneck ada setelah customer masuk.

## Benchmark history - not current policy

- 2023 target: SR 40%, contribution to RS 0.55%.
- 2024 plan: SR 40%, contribution 0.83%, conversion 25%, 2x/month.
- 2025 weak-dealer expectation: minimum 3 deals/event.

Untuk pertanyaan target **sekarang**, jangan pakai angka ini tanpa source 2026/current authority.
