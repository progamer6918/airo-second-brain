# Integrated Network Sustainability (INS)

> **10 detik:** INS mengecek apakah dealer/network bukan cuma jualan, tapi **bisa sustain** dari sisi performance, owner/people, financial, service, customer dan digital capability.

## Lima lensa

- **INM** — quantitative performance & potential.
- **INQE** — qualitative early warning: people, internal problem, regeneration, Honda business, financial, non-Honda business.
- **NOS** — service standard.
- **DCSL** — customer satisfaction.
- **SID** — smart-network/digital capability.

## Kenapa penting?

Dealer dengan sales tinggi belum tentu sehat. Risiko succession, owner focus, cash flow, customer experience atau operating capability bisa membuat hasil tidak sustainable.

Sebaliknya, sales lemah tidak otomatis berarti problem promosi. INS memaksa diagnosis melebar sebelum action.

## Development loop

Segmentasikan concern network → integrated review → tentukan development target → alignment lintas bagian → monitor performance → coaching/PICA → monitor progress.

## Currentness

Parameter/weight/category 2023 jangan dianggap current tanpa authority terbaru.
