# Diagnostic - Event Jalan, Tapi Hasil Lemah

## Step 1 - Tentukan bottleneck

| Kondisi | Cek dulu |
|---|---|
| Sepi pengunjung | lokasi, coverage, promosi, database/campaign activation |
| Ramai tapi prospect sedikit | CTA, sales engagement, riding test, lead capture |
| Leads banyak tapi tidak contacted | nomor/contact quality, assignment, follow-up |
| Contacted tinggi, deal rendah | program, stock/indent, finance, product fit, competitor |
| Deal ada tapi report rendah | source coding, SPK/SO, chassis/system update |
| Cost tinggi, output rendah | event format, location quality, conversion chain |

## Step 2 - Validasi eksekusi

Cross-check plan, actual, evidence photo/geotag, system prospect, sales, and cost. A completed event is not automatically an effective event.

## Step 3 - Action

Action harus diarahkan ke stage yang rusak. Jangan selalu menjawab "tambah event" atau "tambah promo".
