# Network Development Review

Gunakan review ini ketika dealer/area lemah dan belum jelas problem-nya berada di **coverage, territory, people/activity, service atau sustainability**.

## 1. Outcome

- RS / M/S / winning area: total, segment, type, territory mana yang lemah?
- bandingkan baseline, target, monthly path dan YTD achievement.

## 2. Channel coverage

- new / replacement / upgrade mana yang terlambat?
- coverage gap muncul di area mana?
- POS/outlet punya capacity dan economics yang sesuai?

## 3. Territory

- siapa owner target setiap kecamatan?
- bagaimana Location / Ring1?
- ada cross-coverage yang menutupi weakness?
- activity benar terjadi di priority territory?

## 4. People / enablement

- quantity & productivity salespeople cukup?
- knowledge/qualification gap?
- tool utilization benar membantu execution atau hanya login?

## 5. Service

- NOS gap ada di Premises, People atau Process?
- mandatory issue?
- evidence dan PICA sudah jelas?

## 6. Sustainability

- owner / succession / internal issue?
- financial health / cash flow / business focus?
- customer satisfaction?
- digital operating capability?

## 7. Action

Pisahkan symptom, tested cause, action, PIC, due date dan recovery metric. Monitor sampai metric dan operating condition benar-benar recover.

Batch03 synthesis from Channel Management, KPI Highlight, My Hero, INS, NOS 2023 and TTM.
