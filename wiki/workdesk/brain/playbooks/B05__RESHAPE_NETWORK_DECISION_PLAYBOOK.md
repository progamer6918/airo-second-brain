# Reshape Network Decision Playbook

## Kapan pakai reshape?

Pakai reshape kalau masalah tidak cukup diselesaikan dengan activity atau coaching biasa, terutama jika menyangkut:

- cost efficiency;
- productivity;
- process bottleneck;
- duplicated work;
- digital/system integration;
- warehouse/logistic flow;
- CRM/people sharing;
- dealer-group centralized operation;
- readiness for EV/loyalty/online-offline business.

## Decision questions

1. Apa before process-nya?
2. Apa after process-nya?
3. Benefit kualitatif apa?
4. Efficiency % atau productivity gain-nya berapa?
5. Berapa dealer sudah implement?
6. Evidence apa?
7. Kalau belum efektif, apakah problemnya process, people, policy, technology, atau change management?
8. Apa timeline next strategy?

## Warning

Implementation status bukan bukti sukses. Reshape harus terbukti lewat benefit dan evidence.
