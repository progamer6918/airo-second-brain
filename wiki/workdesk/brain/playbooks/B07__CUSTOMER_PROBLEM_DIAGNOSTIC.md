# Customer Problem Diagnostic

When customer outcome is weak, ask in this order:

1. **Awareness** - do target customers know us/product/program?
2. **Appeal** - does the proposition fit their job/real need?
3. **Ask** - can they obtain clear information easily?
4. **Act** - is purchase/process convenient, fast and hassle-free?
5. **Advocate** - are satisfied customers becoming loyal/referral sources?

Then inspect:
- CDB quality;
- segment/profile;
- stated vs real vs unstated need;
- lead stage and reason code;
- moment of truth;
- CSAT/NPS where available;
- Joy/Pride/Connection opportunity;
- cross-division dependency.

Output should be a specific customer problem, not "customer kurang tertarik".
