# My Hero — Sales Force Enablement

> **10 detik:** My Hero 2023 adalah digital sales tool untuk **mendistribusikan knowledge, membantu selling, menguji kemampuan, dan memberi manager visibility terhadap capability salespeople**.

## Capability

- product & general knowledge;
- video/article;
- quiz/survey;
- RFC product recommendation;
- Team Report untuk Kacab;
- SENSE / Self Audit NOS;
- content/social-media/product-comparison/brochure tools.

## Management loop

**knowledge → quiz/qualification → manager visibility → focused coaching/training → better execution.**

Jadi login rate saja bukan outcome. Hubungkan usage dengan knowledge quality, quiz result, qualification dan eksekusi di customer.

## Important boundary

Self Audit NOS di My Hero **bukan official NOS audit result** menurut source 2023.
