# Forecast / Target At Risk — Playbook

## Gejala

Forecast dealer/type menunjukkan finish di bawah target.

## Jawaban pertama bukan “tambah activity”

Gunakan urutan ini:

1. **Validate gap** — target, actual MTD, cut-off, finish estimate.
2. **Localize** — area/dealer/type/segment/sales force mana yang paling menarik gap.
3. **Market** — demand/market/share movement dan local evidence.
4. **People** — current vs required headcount/productivity.
5. **Activity** — frequency, target vs actual output, remaining capacity, target user/area.
6. **Supply** — stock, inbound, product mix, allocation.
7. **Commercial/competitor** — only if evidence points there; use program valid for that period.
8. **Revise plan** — action sesuai mechanism, lalu control harian/mingguan.

## Output format

**Fact** — apa gap dan contributor terukur.  
**Hypothesis** — mechanism yang sedang diuji.  
**Evidence needed** — minimum data untuk confirm/reject.  
**PI** — hanya setelah evidence cukup.  
**Action** — intervention sesuai cause/current authority.  
**Control** — bagaimana recovery dibuktikan.

## Guard

Kalau workbook/template/source tidak menyediakan data current, jangan mengisi dengan historical sample atau dummy tanpa label simulasi.
