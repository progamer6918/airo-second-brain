# Negotiation Quick Reference

Before the meeting write four things:

- **BATNA:** what will I do if no agreement?
- **Reservation:** what is the minimum I can accept?
- **Aspiration:** what outcome do I want?
- **ZOPA:** where might both sides overlap?

Influence lenses from the source:
- Social proof.
- Liking.
- Contrast.
- Time Machine / future benefit.
- Higher Purpose.

Use them to communicate an evidence-backed proposal. They do not replace diagnosis or a viable economic case.
