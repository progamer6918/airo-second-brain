# Management Visit Prep — apa yang harus siap sebelum visit

## Cepatnya

Management Visit bukan cuma jadwal kunjungan. Source meminta preparation yang mencakup dealer selection, schedule, route map, dealer profile, comparison data, Q&A, dealer preparation, MD/dealer presentation, and do/don't.

## Urutan persiapan

```text
Dealer selection
↓
Schedule & route
↓
Dealer profile
↓
Dealer comparison data
↓
Q&A preparation
↓
Dealer preparation
↓
MD + dealer presentation
↓
Do & don't check
```

## Dealer profile harus siap menjawab

- sales performance;
- manpower;
- sales composition;
- pricing comparison;
- BTL activity;
- fincoy condition;
- cash-credit movement;
- focus type performance;
- stock and indent;
- competitor condition.

## Q&A yang harus dipersiapkan

Source meminta kesiapan untuk area:

- market condition;
- customer profile;
- community;
- dealer sales performance;
- financial dealer;
- product acceptance;
- product knowledge;
- program evaluation;
- promotion evaluation;
- OJK regulation;
- competitor condition;
- cross-selling;
- sales people and activity;
- showroom decoration and NOS;
- after-sales service.

## Guard

Detail do/don't Management Visit bersifat internal. WorkDesk public cukup menyimpan struktur persiapan; operationally-sensitive talk boundaries harus berada di private/internal layer.
