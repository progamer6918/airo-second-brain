# POS 2026 — quick reference

POS has dedicated H1 and H2 standards. Treat it as a compact satellite operation with its own minimum premises, people and process—not as a normal dealer with half the checklist ignored.

H1 POS includes identity/display/dealing, Kapos and sales-force capacity, MyHero/training, NMS prospecting, product/EV/Customer Apps, negotiation, SPK/consent, credit/indent and follow-up.

H2 POS includes minimum pit/tools/customer facilities, PIC POS AHASS/admin/mechanic, SA-mechanic-FI-cashier flow and service approval/handover.
