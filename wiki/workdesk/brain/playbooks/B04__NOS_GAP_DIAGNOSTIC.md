# Diagnostic — kenapa NOS lemah?

## Premises lemah, Process kuat, People kuat
Kemungkinan capability sudah berjalan tetapi infrastructure/condition/evidence belum memenuhi. Prioritaskan mandatory/safety/customer-impact items sebelum beautification.

## Process lemah, Premises kuat
Facility exists but customer journey/operating discipline is not happening. Check role ownership, workload, NMS adoption, monitoring, follow-up discipline and supervisor coaching.

## People lemah
Check actual-vs-required headcount, independence/role-sharing thresholds, training completion, MyHero/NMS access, and competency evidence. Do not solve a headcount gap with only training.

## High audit %, low final grade
Look for mandatory violations. The mandatory ceiling can dominate the final grade.

## Many N/A
First verify network layer and legitimate N/A criteria. Do not use N/A to hide missing required capability.
