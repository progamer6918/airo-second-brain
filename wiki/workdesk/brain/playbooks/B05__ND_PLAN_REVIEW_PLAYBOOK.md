# ND Plan Review Playbook

## Tujuan

Membuat review area yang tidak berhenti di sales number, tapi menyambungkan market, coverage, people, activity, service, digital, dan strategy.

## Checklist review

### 1. Market & area
- PDRB/top sector.
- CDB vs PDRB mismatch.
- Market contribution.
- Market growth.
- Pareto and concern area.

### 2. Business performance
- H1: market, retail, M/S, 9-segment, cash/credit, EV, competitor.
- H2: UE, SPR, revenue/month, sales ability, customer movement, coverage, LCR.
- H3: parts/oil/accessories/apparel in/out.
- HC3: CSL, retention, leads/CRM.
- Digital: NMS, CE Apps, AstraPay.

### 3. Network & territory
- Dealer/POS/AHASS/competitor mapping.
- Ring/lokasi/pusat keramaian.
- Target channel and actual.
- TTM evaluation by kecamatan and network.

### 4. People/productivity
- SM/SC/mechanic actual vs plan.
- Productivity and turnover.
- NPBT/unit or NPBT/UE where source supports.

### 5. Strategy
- Coverage H1/H23.
- POS/activity points.
- People development.
- Digital/NOS/GBP improvement.
- Financial/regeneration/other issues.

## Output harus berupa PICA yang spesifik

Jangan tulis `tingkatkan aktivitas`. Tulis titik, jumlah, area, PIC, due date, dan target expected impact kalau datanya mendukung.
