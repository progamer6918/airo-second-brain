# CRM / Leads Playbook

## 10 seconds
Use this when there are many customer leads/data but conversion, repeat order or follow-up quality is weak.

## Start here
1. Verify CDB is **Correct, Complete, Clean**.
2. Separate inbound vs outbound lead sources.
3. Confirm owner -> processor -> executor.
4. Count Prospect, Contacted, Pending, Deal, Not Deal.
5. Compute contacted, success, conversion and contribution rates.
6. Find the largest funnel leak.
7. Inspect reason/status, not only totals.
8. Define treatment by customer profile/need and barrier.
9. Monitor re-follow-up/retargeting.
10. Feed outcome back into CDB/insight.

## Do not
- celebrate lead volume if contact rate is weak;
- blame FLP before checking data validity/assignment;
- assume not-deal is permanently dead;
- use a 2024 allocation number as a 2026 current rule without a current source.
