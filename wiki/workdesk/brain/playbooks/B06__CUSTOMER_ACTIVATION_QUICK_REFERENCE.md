# Customer Activation - Quick Reference

## Physical activation

BTL / Exhibition / Roadshow / Showroom Event  
Use when objective is local coverage, crowd, product experience, riding test, direct prospecting and sales.

## Digital activation

VE / Virtual Showroom  
Use when objective is digital reach, product/promo exploration, finance/service access, lead capture and structured follow-up.

## Common brain

Both require:
- target customer/area;
- relevant proposition;
- usable customer journey;
- lead/prospect capture;
- sales follow-up;
- measurable conversion;
- evidence;
- learning loop.

The channel is different. The professional operating logic is connected.
