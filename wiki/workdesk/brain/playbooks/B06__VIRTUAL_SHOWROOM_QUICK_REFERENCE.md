# Virtual Showroom - Quick Reference

The reconstructed feature blueprint covers:

**Entry:** domicile, login, promo code, terms, welcome.  
**Product:** 360 display, variant, features, brochure, price, promo.  
**Finance:** credit/price tools, FINCOY path, trade-in.  
**Experience:** riding-test registration, live contact.  
**H2:** AHASS booking, service tips, programs, facilities.  
**H3:** parts, accessories, apparel, oil.  
**Education:** Techno Zone / engine & technology content.

Use this as a customer-journey checklist. Do not assume every historical feature is still live in 2026.
