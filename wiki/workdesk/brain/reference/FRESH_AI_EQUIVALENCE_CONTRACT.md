# Fresh-AI Functional Equivalence Contract

## Target
A fresh AI consuming only the canonical ASB brain should be able to perform normal professional reasoning **materially equivalently** to an AI that consumed the original baseline sources, without needing the originals for routine work.

## What equivalence means
Fresh AI should preserve:
- concepts and business meaning;
- procedures and operating sequences;
- diagnostic decompositions and cause-quality tests;
- formulas/metrics that are actually supported;
- case lessons with historical boundaries;
- structural/visual logic needed for decisions;
- current-vs-historical authority;
- cross-domain relationships;
- exact provenance back to source when deeper verification is required.

## What equivalence does not mean
- verbatim reproduction of every slide/page;
- pixel-perfect copy of every dense table;
- exact queryability of every historical dealer row;
- inventing data that the source itself omits or contradicts;
- treating historical training examples as current policy.
