# Residual Semantic Gaps

The remaining gap is **not** “20 sources unread”; physical baseline processing is 80/80. The residual is concentrated in bounded fidelity classes:

1. **Source-bounded ambiguity/missing authority** — cannot be fixed by better summarization (e.g. Niguri calendar conflict, missing VE 2026 authority, empty TTFU, missing external workbook).
2. **Workbook execution fidelity** — business logic is understood, but exact recalculation/external-reference parity is not always proven.
3. **Dense table granularity** — operating meaning is preserved, but not every dealer/month row from large meeting decks is structured into canonical memory.
4. **Fine visual detail** — screenshot-heavy material may preserve the visual operating logic without every tiny label being queryable.
5. **Historical/currentness boundaries** — deliberately retained as historical instead of silently promoted to current rules.

These are materially different from “knowledge missing.” Some are deliberate epistemic safeguards.
