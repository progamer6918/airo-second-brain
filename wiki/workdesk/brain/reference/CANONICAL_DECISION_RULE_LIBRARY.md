# Canonical Decision Rule Library — v0.1

## Diagnosis

1. **Localize before diagnosing.** Find the area/product/dealer/segment/funnel/network layer carrying the contribution.
2. **Read sales with stock, in-transit and distribution** when data are available.
3. **A cause must explain what changed.** A factor that existed both before and after a decline is weak unless its magnitude/condition changed.
4. **Use contrast.** Broad external explanations must explain why comparable areas/products behaved differently.
5. **Use magnitude.** Quantify whether the claimed factor can plausibly explain the observed gap.
6. **Countermeasure must counter the mechanism**, not just add activity.
7. **Volume growth does not imply market-share growth.** Always inspect denominator and competitor growth.
8. **High event traffic does not imply conversion.** Diagnose capture → contact → deal → retail attribution stage-by-stage.
9. **High NOS audit % does not guarantee high final grade** if mandatory items fail.
10. **Implementation status is not outcome.** NetDev/Reshape/project claims need before/after, evidence, benefit and next-control loop.

## Planning / supply

11. Deploy target from market/area potential down to dealer and sales-force executors.
12. Treat forecast as feasibility reasoning (Pattern + Unexplained), not passive extrapolation.
13. Align demand, Niguri, distribution and stock with selling capacity and opportunity.
14. Dealer Review is a strategy diary; update it when facts invalidate the plan.

## Pricing / finance

15. Judge competitiveness at component level, not OTR only: DP Real, installment, rate and financing access matter.
16. Financing quality (FID/BA) can restrict sales access through lender selectivity; current lender policy must be verified.

## Customer / CRM

17. CDB quality requires Correct + Complete + Clean before analytics.
18. Separate lead-generation problems from contact, conversion and attribution problems.
19. Customer journey and needs should inform solution design; do not treat product purchase as the whole customer job.

## Network / territory

20. Select the correct network tier before applying NOS standards.
21. Territory/activity should produce coverage, not merely satisfy activity-count administration.
22. Current standards supersede historical network examples; preserve history for lineage only.

## People / leadership

23. Coach when capability/ownership can be developed; direct when the situation requires instruction/control.
24. Diagnose failed change across Vision, Skills, Incentives, Resources and Action Plan before blaming “resistance.”
25. Use negotiation preparation (BATNA, reservation, aspiration, ZOPA) before stakeholder bargaining.
26. Presentation type should follow the decision job: inform, decide, solve problem, generate ideas or sell/persuade.

## Epistemic / currentness

27. Historical cases generate hypotheses and patterns; they do **not** create causal forecasts.
28. A dated rule is not current until the currentness resolver says it is applicable.
29. If source evidence conflicts, return `UNRESOLVED` rather than silently repairing it.
30. Duplicate/aggregator sources add provenance, not duplicate semantic weight.
