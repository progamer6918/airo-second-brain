# Source Dossier - Final Project Guidance ASSDP Advance 2025

Source: `130. Petunjuk Pelaksanaan Final Project Training ASSDP - Advance 2025.pdf`  
Date on source: 5 Nov 2025  
Units: 4 pages.

## Project theme

**“Breakthrough Integrated Strategy & Activity to Develop Weak Area into a Strength Area.”**

This is the clearest statement of what ASSDP Advance expects the supervisor to demonstrate.

## Required project content

### Process Integrated Improvement
Must bring together:
- Demand Management analysis;
- Marketing Strategy;
- Strong Monitoring Plan;
- H1 Channel Management;
- NOS implementation;
- digitalization utilization.

### Expected value
- strong PICA analysis;
- end-to-end strategy covering Plan, Target, Execution, Monitoring and Evaluation;
- implementation of new way of selling / digitalization.

### Allowed project methods
- 8 Step;
- DMAIC;
- Design Thinking.

The document does **not** say one method is always superior. Select method based on the problem structure.

### Presentation expectation
The presentation should:
- create empathy;
- gain curiosity;
- create confidence.

This directly matches the 9-slide presentation logic in the AHM training material.

## Historical administrative facts

The source specifies submission/final-presentation timing into Apr-Jun 2026 and an administrative cost-recovery consequence of Rp4,000,000 per participant for non-compliance. These are historical training administration, not WorkDesk professional rules.
