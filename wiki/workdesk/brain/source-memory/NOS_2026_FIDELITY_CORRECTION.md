## Guest Book structural boundary
The Guest Book functional row at physical row **48** has blank Item/Sub-item hierarchy cells in the **original source**. Preserve this as `SOURCE_STRUCTURAL_BLANK`. A human-facing label such as `Guest Book` may be used only if marked `INFERRED_FUNCTIONAL_LABEL`; it must not be represented as literal source text.

## Mandatory gate logic from workbook formulas
The 2026 workbook scoring logic maps failed mandatory tiers to maximum result:
- failed `P` → maximum **Gold**;
- failed `G P` → maximum **Silver**;
- failed `S G P` → **Bronze**.

The final result takes the worse of the raw audit result and mandatory result. Therefore a high raw score cannot override a failed mandatory gate.
