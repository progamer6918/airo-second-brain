## Core model
Interpersonal skill is framed as the ability to interact, communicate and cooperate effectively and positively. The training covers active listening, effective communication, empathy, conflict resolution, emotional intelligence, assertiveness/respect, collaboration/teamwork, effective meetings and managing the boss.

### Communication
- Communication quality is judged by **impact/understanding at the receiver**, not sender intention alone.
- Separate verbal choice of words, vocal delivery and non-verbal/body-language signals.
- The **Ladder of Inference** warns that visible data can be filtered into meaning, assumptions, conclusions and actions; diagnosis should slow down that reflexive loop.

### Listening / empathy / conflict
- Common listening failure modes: self-focus, prior prejudice and partial listening.
- Empathy means understanding the other person's position and builds support/engagement/teamwork.
- Conflict resolution combines empathetic listening, finding common ground and fair win-win compromise.
- The source teaches `3A`: Acceptance, Approval, Appreciation.

### Emotional intelligence / assertiveness
The training connects self-awareness, self-regulation, empathy and social skill to healthier communication. Assertiveness is positioned between over-yielding/passive and egoistic/aggressive communication: clear/direct while respecting self and others.

### Meetings
Six effective-meeting controls are shown: preparation/agenda, clear objective, relevant participants, sufficient but controlled time, openness/collaboration, and documentation/follow-up.

### Managing your boss
Understand pressure, work/communication style, strengths/weaknesses/blind spots, expectations and goals. Maintain information flow and dependability/honesty; be proactive and solution-oriented.
