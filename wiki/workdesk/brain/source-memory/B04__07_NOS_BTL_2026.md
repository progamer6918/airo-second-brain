# NOS BTL 2026

**Source:** `07. NOS BTL 2026.xlsx`

BTL has its own temporary-event NOS for H1 and H23. It preserves the operating standard even outside a permanent network facility.

## H1 BTL
Premises: tent/carpet/event area, dealing setup, unit display, riding test, current price/program material, MyHero.
People: minimum sales people, training, appearance.
Process: needs discovery, product/Customer Apps, accessories/apparel, negotiation, NMS/SPK/data consent, indent/credit/documents, event follow-up.

## H23 BTL
Premises/tools: tent, service form, pit replacement board, mechanic truster, service stock container, promo material.
People: minimum mechanic, TTL 1, standard uniform/PPE.
Process: arrival, consultation, work approval, extra work approval, repair, final inspection, handover and payment.

## Decision rule
Do not audit an event with permanent-dealer NOS alone. Use the BTL context when the activity is a BTL/exhibition/roadshow service/sales operation.
