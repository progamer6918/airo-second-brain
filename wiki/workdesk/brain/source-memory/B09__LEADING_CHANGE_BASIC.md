## Change frame
The leader's role is framed as achieving the right results while overcoming organizational inertia/complacency. Agility is linked to **speed** across People, Process and Technology, plus collaboration that enables new ways of serving customers.

## Personal resilience
The `10,000 No` story is used to teach persistence after rejection. The sequence is:
- reframe a rejection as learning;
- maintain perseverance;
- recognize transformation periods that feel like failure;
- protect belief/faith in the next action.

`Soundtracks` is the source's metaphor for repeated internal narratives. The practical use is to notice broken narratives and deliberately replace/reframe them instead of letting them drive behavior automatically.

## Systematic change
The deck reuses DMAIC:
Define -> Measure -> Analyze -> Improve -> Control.
This creates a bridge between motivational change leadership and measurable continuous improvement.

## Persuasive communication
The source reinforces listening, questioning, appreciation/3A, letting the other party fully state the issue, and avoiding argument escalation. `Think like a fish` is the source's shorthand for designing persuasion from the other party's perspective, not the persuader's preference.

## Boundary
Motivational language is training framing, not a guarantee that belief or persistence alone creates business results. WorkDesk should always reconnect change to evidence, capability, resources, process and follow-up.
