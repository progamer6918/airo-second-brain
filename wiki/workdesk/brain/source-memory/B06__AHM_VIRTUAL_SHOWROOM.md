# Source Dossier - AHM Virtual Showroom / Virtual Exhibition Feature Architecture

**Source:** `AHM-Showroom-I (1).pptx`, 28 slides.

## What this source contributes

The deck is not a sales-performance report. It is a customer-experience / platform feature blueprint showing how a digital showroom can reproduce and connect H1, H2 and H3 experiences.

## Customer path

`landing -> login/domicile/promo code -> terms -> bumper/welcome -> choose Virtual Showroom or Virtual Exhibition -> navigate product/service zones`.

## H1/product experience

The platform can expose:
- motorcycle category/zone;
- 360-degree product display and variants;
- product feature hotspots;
- price;
- brochure;
- price list;
- promo;
- RFC/calculator;
- trade-in form;
- riding-test registration.

## H2/AHASS experience

The platform also represents:
- AHASS facility / waiting-room visibility;
- service tips;
- AHASS programs;
- booking service;
- technical/Techno Zone content;
- live chat / information support.

## H3/parts-accessories experience

Parts, accessories, apparel, helmet and oil areas are included with product visualisation/downloadable information.

## Strategic meaning

Virtual customer experience is designed as **one connected customer journey**, not merely a digital motorcycle brochure. A good VE/virtual-showroom assessment should therefore ask whether product information, finance, service, riding test, after-sales, human support and lead capture are connected coherently.
