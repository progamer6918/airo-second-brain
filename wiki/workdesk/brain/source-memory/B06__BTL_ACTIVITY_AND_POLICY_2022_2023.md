## Why BTL exists

Across the sources, BTL is positioned as a response to:
- changing customer behaviour;
- aggressive competitor sales programs/BTL;
- new competitor products/promotions;
- need to improve retail and market share, especially through area coverage.

## Three distinct activity types

### Exhibition / Pameran
Historical standard includes potential location, unit display, sufficient sales people, dealing setup, sales-program material and evidence. 2022 letter required at least two different exhibition points and three days per point.

### Roadshow
Not just a larger exhibition. It requires crowd-generating/hard-selling content such as entertainment, games/competition and riding test. The source repeatedly distinguishes a roadshow from an ordinary static display.

### Showroom Event
Dealer-premise activity with event content, e.g. invited potential customers, live music/social content, exercise, thematic day. Historical guidance expected at least one showroom event per month.

## Coverage principle

A key Activity BTL takeaway is that exhibition/roadshow should serve **coverage**, rather than being performed at the dealer frontage and then labelled as market activation. Showroom Event is the explicit dealer-premise exception.

The source also encourages joint activity with H2/AHASS to generate crowd and with finance companies to maximise sales opportunity.

## Planning -> approval -> execution -> evidence -> claim

Historical 2023 sponsorship process:
1. Dealer submits M+1 activity proposal to MD by the stated M-1 deadline (illustrated as the 4th).
2. MD checks/approves support eligibility.
3. Dealer executes the approved event.
4. Dealer submits LPJ by the stated M+1 deadline (illustrated as the 2nd).
5. LPJ includes cost evidence and event photos.
6. MD approves LPJ before dealer submits claim documents.

This is a control system, not paperwork after the fact: proposal and actual must match or support can be rejected.

## LPJ performance memory

Source expects LPJ to contain items such as:
- duration/date;
- location/address;
- estimated and actual visitors;
- sales;
- riding-test participants;
- hot prospects;
- sales from hot prospects;
- percentage conversion from hot prospects;
- actual cost;
- supporting evidence.

Historical rule states recognised event sales are sales occurring at the event/location during the event period and supported by SPK/down-payment evidence.

## Evidence requirements

Examples repeatedly required:
- overall activity setup/unit display;
- sales people serving visitors;
- visitor/crowd condition;
- riding-test activity;
- cost receipts/notes;
- geotag/location/time evidence in the 2022 instruction.

## Historical sponsorship terms - do not use as current

The 2023 program stated:
- exhibition MD contribution 32.5%, max Rp800,000/event point before VAT;
- roadshow contribution 35%, max Rp900,000/event point before VAT;
- historical PPh 23 handling of 2% in that program.

These are dated commercial terms and **must not be reused for 2026 claims** without current policy.
