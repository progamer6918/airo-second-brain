## 2025 NMS lead process

Source flow:
1. Customer accesses the VE site, registers and enters dealer voucher/code.
2. Customer selects **Saya Tertarik** and product/dealer information.
3. Main Dealer assigns the lead.
4. Dealer CRM/Admin opens NMS `H1 -> Prospek -> Leads` and assigns it to FLP.
5. FLP opens `Need Follow Up`, follows and updates the prospect until deal/non-deal resolution.
6. CRM/Admin H1 opens a deal lead, creates SPK and continues through Sales Order.

### Important definition change

The source says that **starting March 2025, VE deal result is counted by chassis number (nomor rangka), not phone number**. This is intended to improve deal verification and requires dealers to complete the NMS flow correctly so the chassis number appears.

The slide also sets a 2025 operational expectation of **minimum 3 deals per VE event** for dealers with weak contribution. This is dated 2025 guidance.

## Survey design

Survey period: 1-8 March 2025.  
Respondents: 124.

Purpose:
- measure VE effectiveness;
- understand customer content preferences;
- gather improvement input.

## Reported customer findings

### Demography
- Age 31-40: 46 (37%).
- Age 20-30: 43 (35%).
- Age 41-50: 18 (15%).
- Age <20: 9 (7%).
- Age >50: 8 (6%).
- Private employees: 35 (28%).
- Entrepreneurs: 26 (21%).
- Farmers: 24 (19%).

### Source of VE awareness - as reported
- Offline dealer visit: 49 (40%).
- Social media: 40 (32%).
- WhatsApp blast: 9 (7%).
- Other: 15 (12%).

The listed counts do not fully reconcile to n=124. The reconstruction preserves the source as reported rather than silently correcting it.

### Why customers use VE
- Attractive promotion: 48 (39%).
- Product information: 48 (39%).
- Digital experience: 20 (16%).
- Other: 8 (6%).

### Most attractive content
- Special promo/discount: 44 (35%).
- Product specification: 25 (20%).
- Credit simulation: 14 (11%).
- Other content: 41 (34%).

### Less attractive / friction
- Navigation/ease of use: 43 (35%).
- Slow response: 27 (22%).
- Visual design: 21 (17%).
- Remaining responses are grouped as other in the chart.

### Promo expectations
- Direct discount: 36 (29%).
- Low installment/DP: 30 (24%).
- Accessory bonus: 11 (9%).

## Source-grounded implication

The customer sees VE as a combination of **promotion + product information + dealer response**, not merely a virtual booth. The 2025 research therefore reinforces the funnel logic from older VE guidance: UX friction and follow-up speed can destroy value even when traffic/leads exist.
