## Semantic memory
The instruction standardizes daily and event appearance for Front Line People (Sales People) across five clothing modes: FLP uniform, Event uniform, Dealer uniform, official Honda apparel, and Batik/local attire.

- **FLP uniform:** red/white-collar shirt; black trousers or black skirt; black formal shoes/sneakers, with the source also allowing 5 cm heels for women.
- **Event uniform:** black Wing Mark Honda polo, khaki chinos, black/white/tonal sneakers.
- **Dealer uniform:** dealer shirt/polo; the design may not use Honda motorcycle product logos or non-Honda brand logos; trousers/chinos or non-faded/non-torn jeans; sneakers.
- **Official Honda apparel:** must be official AHM apparel and thematically fit the event/product. The source explicitly gives HRC apparel at a Scoopy event as a mismatch example.
- **Batik/local attire:** batik or local-region attire with matching lower garment and shoes.

ID Card is mandatory across uniforms. Accessories, make-up and detailed grooming defer to the FLP appearance/grooming standard.

## Location/event matrix
The source distinguishes Showroom, Main Dealer Event and Dealer Event. FLP uniform and official Honda apparel can cover all three; Event uniform is for Main Dealer events; Dealer uniform covers showroom/dealer-event use; Batik is shown for showroom use.

## Decision use
Use this as an appearance-compliance reference only after a currentness check. Do not infer broader NOS service quality from uniform compliance alone.
