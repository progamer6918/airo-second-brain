## Core idea

INS treats dealer/network sustainability as a **multi-dimensional system**, not as sales volume alone. The background is rising cost/resource pressure, the need for profit and competitiveness, and the requirement that network development improve customer satisfaction as well as financial sustainability. (slide 2)

## Five lenses

1. **INM — performance & potential**  
   Quantitative network performance/potential across H1/H2/H3/HC3 parameter families. The examples include retail sales, market share, people quantity/productivity, activities, distribution, PO/Niguri discipline, repeat order, parts/oil/accessory economics, retention and related measures. (slides 3, 5–6)

2. **INQE — qualitative sustainability / early warning**  
   Qualitative view of people, internal problems, regeneration, Honda business, financial condition, and non-Honda business/financial exposure. This explicitly brings owner succession, family/internal issues, focus, business health, cash flow and other-business risk into network diagnosis. (slides 3, 7)

3. **NOS — service standard**  
   Premises, process and people: whether the network delivers the expected customer experience. (slide 3)

4. **DCSL — customer satisfaction**  
   Customer viewpoint used alongside internal/network measures. (slide 3)

5. **SID — smart-network/digitalization**  
   Digital/tool utilization dimensions such as Smart Operation, Smart Brain, Smart Engagement and Smart Partnership. (slides 3, 8)

## From score to development action

The source uses network categories such as **Supreme, Moderate and Watchout** to focus development attention. The useful concept is triage:

- strong network → maintain strengths and pilot/develop remaining weak parameters;
- moderate network → monitor, evaluate and improve to move upward;
- watchout network → MD concern; focus integrated review on the items causing weakness. (slide 9)

The operating rhythm described is quarterly INS submission/targeting, monthly network integrated review, scheduled AHM–MD–Dealer integrated review, and development actions such as target-setting, cross-functional alignment, performance monitoring, coaching/counseling with PICA and progress monitoring. (slides 10–11)

## Diagnostic rule

A dealer can be commercially strong yet structurally fragile. INS therefore asks: **Is performance sustainable given owner/people succession, financial health, customer experience and operating capability?**

Conversely, a low commercial result should not be diagnosed only as a sales problem if INQE/NOS/DCSL/SID reveal deeper constraints.
