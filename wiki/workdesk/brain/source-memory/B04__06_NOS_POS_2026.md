# NOS POS 2026

**Source:** `06. NOS POS 2026.xlsx`

POS is a distinct operating context, not a miniature dealer checklist copied blindly. The workbook separates H1 sales POS and H2/AHASS POS.

## H1 POS
Premises: POS identity/sign shop, dealing table, X-banner, display, parking/signage, operating hour, optional apparel.

People: Kepala POS, Salesman/Sales Counter, training, MyHero/sales tools, and sales-force capacity logic. The workbook provides a target headcount model using POS sales target, contribution handled by SM/SC, senior/junior productivity, and senior/junior mix.

Process: appointment/prospecting through NMS/NMS Mobile, needs discovery, EV awareness, product explanation, Customer Apps, accessories/apparel, riding test, negotiation, SPK/data consent, credit/indent, document status, delivery, leaving/follow-up.

## H2 POS
Premises/tools: exterior/interior, pit, piping, disposal, customer waiting, service price, APAR, service tools.

People: PIC POS AHASS, admin, mechanic; role-sharing rules and minimum training.

Process: compact SA → mechanic → FI → cashier service journey, including estimates, approval for additional work, final inspection and handover.
