Method: DMAIC.

## Define - the Merangin paradox

FY2025 baseline from source:
- Merangin market contribution: about 13% of Jambi provincial market, rank #2 by volume contribution.
- Honda M/S: **79.64%**, rank #7 / third-lowest among nine kabupaten/kota.
- Provincial Honda M/S benchmark: **81.09%**.

The case frames this as high-volume but under-penetrated: boosting Merangin share can materially affect provincial share.

## Customer/economic context

Source says about **59%** of Merangin consumer base is farmers (sawit/karet context). The project hypothesis is that buying decisions are often shaped in community environments before a customer reaches the dealer.

Strategic implication: presence must move closer to where decisions form, not rely only on dealer walk-in.

## Target logic

Historical project targets:
- Jan M/S 80.0%;
- Feb 80.5%;
- Mar 81.0%.

Projected market / Honda target in source:
- Jan: 1,402 market -> 1,122 Honda target;
- Feb: 1,722 -> 1,386;
- Mar: 2,147 -> 1,739.

Dealer target is then broken down to TDM Bangko, CLS Bangko, Anugrah and CLS Pamenang.

Guard: these are project targets derived from FY2025/Q1 assumptions, not current universal forecasts.

## Measure

FY2025 monthly average:
- Honda 1,117 units / **79.64%** M/S / +26.6% vs 2024;
- Yamaha 278 / 19.83% / +31.8%;
- total market 1,402 / +27.6%.

Key contradiction: Honda volume grew strongly, but the market/competitor grew slightly faster.

Sales-source observations:
- Sales Counter Walk-in: **86.99%**;
- Social Media: 1.57%;
- AHASS waiting-room: **1.11%**;
- Canvassing under Salesman source: **58.02%**;
- Roadshow: 6.32%;
- other categories as source-reported.

## Analyze - PICA root causes

Three root-cause themes:
1. **Buying Behavior** - decisions are shaped before dealer arrival; farmer/community presence can be stronger.
2. **Community Activation** - canvassing is productive but coverage/intensity can be systematized.
3. **AHASS Opportunity** - waiting-room customer is a trusted, captive prospect pool but contribution was only 1.11%.

## Improve - three pillars

### Pillar 1 - Hyper-local Branding / Warung Presence
- activate warung/toko/UMKM owners as contact points;
- hyper-visible branding and salesman contact;
- move brand presence upstream of purchase decision.

### Pillar 2 - Integrated Community Engagement
- structured territory canvassing with PIC and mapped potential areas;
- community/event presence as participation, not only hard selling.

### Pillar 3 - AHASS Strike Program
- rotating Sales Counter follow-up in waiting room;
- visual selling/promo communication;
- prospect tracking and follow-up.

Synergy rule: the pillars answer different root causes and should reinforce one another rather than operate as three unrelated events.

## Control

Daily:
- sales dashboard by dealer and salesperson.

Monthly:
- dealer review;
- market condition;
- dealer M/S + volume vs target;
- field activity coverage/frequency;
- AHASS follow-up consistency;
- documentation;
- next action.

## Reported results / source cautions

Page 18 reports:
- Jan target 80.0%, actual M/S **82.85%** and market Honda figure **2,039 units**;
- Feb target 80.5%, actual M/S **80.40%** and market Honda figure **1,624 units**;
- March market actual is shown as **TBD** in the target-vs-actual market panel.

The same page separately shows dealer sales actual totals:
- Jan 1,567 vs dealer target 1,122;
- Feb 1,559 vs 1,386;
- Mar 1,543 vs 1,739.

Do not equate the market-Honda figures with dealer-sales totals without a measure definition; the page is evidently presenting different measures.

The narrative also says “aggregate Q1 2026 M/S 80.8%” while the March market panel remains TBD. Preserve this as a **source internal timing ambiguity** rather than silently treating 80.8% as fully closed Q1 market evidence.

## Reusable lesson

`market paradox -> customer/economic context -> quantified target -> route to root causes -> integrated interventions -> daily/monthly monitoring -> evaluate and adapt`.

The reusable asset is the reasoning architecture, not the expectation that the same three activities will reproduce the same effect elsewhere.
