## Problem
The source says lead follow-up was a major dealer-sales backbone, averaging about 36% contribution in the cited historical context, yet LOL lead-management performance was weak. The project problem statement reports roughly:
- Contacted Rate below 65%
- Success Rate around 0.3%
- fewer than 100 deals/month across 61 dealers

The operational causes include weak dealer/PIC CRM monitoring, many lead types to manage and lack of a simple daily control surface.

## Management system
- determine lead capacity per dealer based on follow-up capacity, salesperson count and working days;
- probe/profile CDB data using analytical filtering (the case mentions KNIME and five years of historical CDB);
- train follow-up skill and scripts, including a Golden Time concept;
- create PIC CRM / dealer booster competition around Contacted Rate, Closing/Success and sales from leads;
- synchronize daily MD-Dealer monitoring in a live spreadsheet/MonitorMu-style control;
- evaluate daily and monthly and adjust treatment/program by dealer performance.

## Reported result
For Oct 2022-Feb 2023 the deck reports Contacted Rate improvement, Success Rate reaching about 1.4%, and average deals around 230 units/month, substantially above the cited starting condition.

Use this as a **lead-operations case**: allocation -> profiling -> skill -> daily visibility -> intervention -> evaluation. Never reuse its uplift as a forecast.
