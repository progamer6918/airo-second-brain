## Final-project contract
After ASSDP training and the HSO Denpasar benchmark, the final presentation must demonstrate development of a network project using both the soft and hard competencies learned in the program.

The source explicitly requires **DMAIC** in both project-presentation structure and presentation delivery. The final deck was due January 29, 2024 and scheduled for online presentation in February 2024.

Semantic rule retained: a final improvement project should not be a photo-report of activity. It should show a systematic problem-to-control story and demonstrate that the learning was applied in network execution.
