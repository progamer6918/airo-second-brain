## What BEST is in WorkDesk terms

BEST is a monthly operating forum that turns strategy into dealer execution controls. It is not only sales announcement. Across months it repeatedly ties together:

- calendar of events and thematic activity;
- BTL/exhibition/roadshow execution and accuracy;
- EV introduction and activation;
- register motor vs SSU accuracy;
- register user;
- SINSENGO/NMS leads handling;
- MSW/sales support socialization;
- indent/logistics/PDI digital;
- CRM/CDB/mystery/calling/booking;
- TTM/KPB dashboard and H2/H3 controls;
- people development, SEIN, MyHero, SIPEDE;
- content/social-media standards;
- business-control/compliance cases.

## Repeated control loops

The repeat across monthly BEST sources indicates that WorkDesk should treat these as operating loops:

1. **Plan activity:** calendar/theme/program/area target.
2. **Execute:** BTL, showroom event, roadshow, social content, EV activation.
3. **Record in system:** SEEDS, NMS, webconsole, PDI digital, proposal/actual BTL.
4. **Check accuracy:** actual BTL location, register motor vs SSU, register user, indent accuracy, lead status.
5. **Convert:** prospect -> SPK -> SSU, leads -> assign sales -> follow-up -> deal.
6. **Review quality:** mystery calling/booking, CDB quality, social-media/branding/NOS grooming, people capability.
7. **Take action:** dealer confirmation, monitoring by Kacab/CRM/Area, PICA/TTFU, next-month theme.

## Currentness guard

Do not answer a current sales support/program question from a historical BEST month if the query date belongs to another month. BEST is a monthly operating-memory source. Use the latest applicable month and MSW/currentness resolver.
