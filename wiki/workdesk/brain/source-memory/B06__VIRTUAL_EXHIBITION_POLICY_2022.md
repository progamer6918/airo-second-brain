# Source Dossier - Virtual Exhibition Leads Policy 2022

**Sources:**  
- `Virtual Exhibition (+SAFARI) - April 2022.pdf` - 17 pages.  
- `Guidance VE to MD (Reg Meeting 18 - 19 July 2022).pdf` - 11 pages.

**Authority:** historical 2022 VE policy and evaluation guidance.

## Lead taxonomy is part of the data model

The source distinguishes at least:
- Independent VE;
- Finance-company VE;
- Product launching VE;
- LOL/sales-program sources.

The source code must correspond to the actual platform/event. Combining different finance-company events under one generic VE source prevents event-level evaluation.

## Invited vs non-invited

- **Invited customer:** customer database/RO supplied or blasted to enter the VE platform.
- **Non-invited customer:** free user who enters without invitation.

This matters because acquisition method and campaign effectiveness cannot be evaluated correctly if source identity is lost.

## Triple-lead / deduplication policy

Customer actions such as **Saya Tertarik**, **Hubungi Sales**, and **Motor Impian** can create multiple raw records. The 2022 guidance requires MD filtering before upload so repeated activity by the same customer does not become fake lead volume. The source illustrates filtering by customer identity such as name and phone number before upload to MonitorMu.

**Decision rule:** raw clicks are not equivalent to unique leads.

## Funnel and SLA

The operating funnel is:

`Leads -> Contacted -> Prospect -> Deal`.

Historical 2022 SLA categories shown in the source:
- Hubungi Sales: 3 minutes.
- Saya Tertarik: 3 hours.
- Motor Impian: 24 hours.

The deck also shows an historical first dealer follow-up condition around 43.9 hours in Q1 2022 and explicitly flags it as needing improvement. These numbers are diagnostic history, not current SLA.

## Evaluation requirement

July 2022 guidance requires MD to evaluate:
- lead input completeness;
- cleansing/category accuracy;
- Independent vs FINCOY vs Product Launch performance;
- strategy and promotion;
- event plan vs actual and marketing evidence.

A VE is therefore not complete when the platform closes. It is complete only when the captured funnel can be reconciled and learned from.
