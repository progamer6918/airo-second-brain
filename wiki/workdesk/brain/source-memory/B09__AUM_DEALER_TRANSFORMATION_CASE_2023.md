## Starting condition
The dealer describes itself as an older/conventional network that faced pandemic disruption, loss of a leader, relocation pressure and unstable/drop sales. The organization/team condition and the cost of maintaining the dealer became explicit problems.

## Transformation pattern
The response is multi-system rather than a single sales program:
- long-term roadmap and target communication;
- remuneration/reward and SOP/reward-punishment changes;
- clearer leadership and decision flow;
- people placement and work-environment improvements;
- digital/technology adoption and direct capability development;
- permanent facility, warehouse and supporting-asset investment;
- creative sales/activity/branding and salespeople incentives;
- active synergy with Main Dealer, fincoy and other stakeholders.

## Reported outcomes
The deck reports recovery of sales into the 430s with a high around 470, Unit Entry above 2,500 with a high around 2,700, and multiple service/productivity/skill achievements in 2023.

## Reusable lesson
Use as a **dealer-transformation case pattern**: when the problem is structural, combine leadership, people, process, assets, digital capability, sales activity and stakeholder support. Do not treat any one practice as proven causal on its own.
