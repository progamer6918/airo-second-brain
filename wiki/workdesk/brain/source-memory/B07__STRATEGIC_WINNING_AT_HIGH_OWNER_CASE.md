# Source Dossier - Strategic Winning AT High Through Boosting Brand Awareness

**Source:** `Strategic Winning AT High Through Boosting Brand Awareness.pdf`  
**Owner applied case:** Merangin, results Oct-Dec 2024, presentation dated 2025.

## Problem framing

The project addresses underperformance in Honda AT High in Kabupaten Merangin. YTD Sep 2024 market data shown:

- Honda AT High: 54 units, 28.5% share.
- Yamaha: 134 units, 71.0% share.
- Total market: 189.

YTD Sep 2023 -> YTD Sep 2024 comparison shown:
- Honda 360 -> 484 (+34%).
- Yamaha 764 -> 1,207 (+58%).
- Total market 1,128 -> 1,698 (+51%).
- Honda share 32% -> 29% (-3 pp), despite Honda volume growth.

This is a strong diagnostic example: **volume growth does not guarantee market-share improvement when the denominator/competitor grows faster**.

## Market insight

Macro/micro analysis ties purchasing power to rubber/palm commodity cycles, infrastructure, credit conditions, local harvest timing, low brand awareness in remote areas, and community/product/financing opportunity. Economic-driver mapping is done by kecamatan.

## Target breakdown

Historical project targets:
- Target AT High M/S: Oct 30%, Nov 32.5%, Dec 35%.
- Target retail: Oct 94, Nov 84, Dec 129.
- Four focus dealers: CLS Bangko, Tunas Bangko, Anugrah Honda Motor, CLS Pamenang.

Targets are further broken down by type and dealer/coverage.

## Execution

Three strategy families are shown:

1. **Touring Program & Roadshow Festival** - Bangko-Jangkat route, test ride, promotion, community involvement. KPI: participants and sales generated during the event.
2. **ATL & BTL promotion** - dealer branding plus traditional market/showroom activity. KPI: showroom visitor volume and conversion.
3. **CSR initiative** - local social/community activities. KPI: number of social activities.

## Monitoring

Weekly:
- activity and field-program evaluation.

Monthly:
- target-vs-actual sales analysis;
- market-share growth;
- strategy-effectiveness review;
- dealer/PIC effectiveness and dealer contribution.

## Reported results

Historical reported project results:
- M/S Oct 30.6%, Nov 44.6%, Dec 40.6% vs project target ending at 35%.
- Actual retail: Oct 95, Nov 114, Dec 138.
- Touring/Roadshow: 100 participants, 5 unit direct contribution during event, 10 hot prospects.
- ATL/BTL: reported showroom walk-in increase 30%.
- CSR: 3 social activities executed.

## Learning value

This is case memory, not a reusable forecast model. Its durable value is the chain:

`market-share diagnosis -> economic/territory insight -> target breakdown -> dealer/type/coverage focus -> activation -> weekly/monthly monitoring -> reported outcome`.
