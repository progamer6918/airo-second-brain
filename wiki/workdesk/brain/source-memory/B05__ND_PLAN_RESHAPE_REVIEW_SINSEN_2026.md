## What this source is

This deck is not just a presentation template. It is an operating checklist for how a Main Dealer must review **Network Development Plan** and **Reshape Network** in 2026. It forces analysis to move from macro market drivers into area performance, network mapping, people productivity, network sustainability, reshape efficiency, and measurable PICA.

## Required thinking flow

1. **Main Dealer profile** sets context.
2. **Market Driven (PDRB) and Info by Area** compares top economic sectors across years and areas; the source explicitly asks for pareto areas and concern areas.
3. **PDRB vs CDB by Area** tests whether actual customer database exposure matches economic drivers. If top CDB sectors do not cover top PDRB sectors, the area may still be potential or underdeveloped.
4. **Top 3 Market Driven & Information** requires 2-3 year quantitative support such as NTP trend and positive/negative sector information.
5. **Quarterly Market Information** translates sector data into practical timing/context: harvest, commodity price, school holiday, government project, weather risk, etc.
6. **Business Milestone** requires H1, H2, H3, HC3, and Development KPIs over 2019-2026/YTD comparison.
7. **Position Audit by Area** compares YTD 2026 vs YTD 2025, not only total MD.
8. **H1/H2/H3/HC3/Digitalization evaluations** require trend graphs, explanation, and strategy/PICA.
9. **Mapping Area (Sustainability POV)** uses Strong/Optimize/Develop/Weak classifications and asks for PICA where actual strong-area achievement is below target.
10. **Market contribution x market growth quadrant** is used to prioritize two review areas.
11. Each selected area is then reviewed end-to-end: market/integrated performance, mapping network, mapping ring, TTM evaluation, people productivity, strategy, and network-level roadmap.
12. Reshape review then evaluates cost efficiency, sales/UE productivity, network transformation, sharing function, shared logistics, incubate reshape, and MD transformation.

## Deep semantics

The deck establishes that **network development is not about adding outlets blindly**. A network point is evaluated through the combination of:

- market/economic potential;
- actual Honda and competitor presence;
- H1/H2/H3/HC3 business performance;
- digitalization readiness;
- coverage sufficiency;
- area sustainability;
- people productivity;
- cost efficiency;
- ability to execute transformation and sharing function.
