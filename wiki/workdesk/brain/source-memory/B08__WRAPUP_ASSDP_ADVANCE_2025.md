## Agenda / integrated operating system

The wrap-up connects five operational blocks:
1. Demand Management.
2. Marketing Sense / Marketing Strategy.
3. Sales & Stock Monitoring / PICA.
4. H1 Channel / Territory Management.
5. Network Operational Standard.
6. Digital implementation is then presented as the operating enablement layer.

## Demand -> Forecast -> Strategy - slides 3-12

Demand Management must answer **why**, not just describe what happened.

Forecasting combines pattern with unexplained factors. The process links potential market, economic condition, regional economics and motorcycle market-prediction factors.

Marketing strategy asks:
- which product/segment will be penetrated and how much volume;
- what activity fits the target;
- which support strategy (price/place/skill/etc.) enables penetration;
- whether execution was effective;
- whether the approach can be expanded to another segment.

Targeting flow:
`identify market potential -> historical trend -> area mapping -> sales target -> SF/productivity planning -> activity planning`.

## Strong monitoring / PICA - slides 13-24

Sales & Stock monitoring descends:
`Total MD -> Kabupaten -> Dealer`.

PICA Retail Sales can decompose:
- Area -> Product;
- Product -> Area.

PICA Market Share:
- identify pareto subsegment contribution;
- isolate subsegments whose M/S decline drives total decline;
- then continue to deeper cause analysis.

## Channel / territory - slides 25-31

Channel Management logic includes:
- coverage gap vs dealer performance;
- blank area / resign / feasibility of dealer/POS;
- productivity improvement;
- replacement dealer;
- cross coverage / temporary exhibition where appropriate.

The planning slide shows a staged new/upgrade process from location, proposal/layout, permits, tools, renovation, people, SP3D and ODA-in. Treat month placement as a historical example, not universal lead time.

Territory Management uses horizontal/vertical target and ring/area coverage. The shown ring distances/weights are historical 2025 training values, not current universal rule.

Activity plan joins:
`dealer activity + salespeople productivity + daily sales + planned activity + people composition`.

## NOS / digital - slides 32-43

NOS is shown through H1 customer journey, mandatory premises/process/people and implementation/result/benefit. Current 2026 NOS authority supersedes this training snapshot for current operational questions.

Digital slides show lead/transaction/follow-up/salesman activity control as an enablement layer for execution and measurement.

## Synthesis

The deck's real value is the integrated chain:

`Demand -> Marketing Strategy -> Target -> Sales/Stock Monitoring -> PICA -> Channel/Territory -> NOS -> Digital Control`.
