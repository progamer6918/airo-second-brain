## Core model

CRM is framed as a business strategy for managing and strengthening customer relationships using strategy, activities and technology. The stated business problem is not merely communication: competitive markets require retention, personalized treatment, use of customer data, repeat order and loyalty.

The source's CRM milestone is:

`Customer Database Management -> Customer Insight Management -> Program Management -> Towards Customer Loyalty`.

That is important because the database is not the end product. Data is acquired and cleaned so it can become insight and then a relevant treatment/program.

## CDB quality

The source defines **3C Quality CDB**:

- **Correct** - accurate, consistent with reality, internally consistent.
- **Complete** - no required data missing; adequate coverage.
- **Clean** - no duplication and no avoidable input errors.

CDB flow is represented as collection -> standardization -> cleansing -> utilization/quality, with dealer, Main Dealer and AHM all having acquisition/verification/utilization roles.

KPI examples shown: quantity CDB/USTK, KK, email, CDB Quality, NIK validity, data updating and Flag 2. These are historical source indicators; do not assume every field remains current.

## Leads system

The workshop separates:

- **Outbound leads**: generated from existing database / cross-selling / LOL / invited VE / fincoy and similar sources.
- **Inbound leads**: generated from consumer touchpoints such as social media, non-invited VE, mail, mobile apps, chatbot, BTL, walk-in, call.

Shared logic:

`generate/capture -> validate/process -> dispatch -> follow up/probe -> dealing -> monitor/evaluate`.

Core performance indicators stated:

- Conversion Rate = Deal / Total Leads.
- SLA.
- Contribution to Sales = Deal / Total Sales.
- Contacted Rate = Contacted / Prospect.
- Success Rate = Deal / Contacted.

## Follow-up guidance shown in 2024 source

The detailed slide includes: redundant-data checks, RO vs non-RO routing, assignment to FLP/SC/SM, three contact attempts, call/WA/visit/email options, follow-up based on golden time, pending/not-deal recycling, and status updates. It also shows historical daily allocation guidance of 5 leads to Salesman and 10 to Sales Counter.

**Currentness rule:** those quantities, tool names and exact follow-up steps are dated 2024 training guidance. Preserve them as process memory, not automatic current policy.

## Lead status language

The source distinguishes Suspect, Prospect, Dispatch/Assign, Contacted/Not Contacted, Pending (Hot/Medium/Low/Inden), and Deal/Sales/Not Deal. It also documents source-specific definitions for AHM MPA, CC, VE and customer apps.

Pending timing in this source:
- Hot: <2 weeks.
- Medium: 2-4 weeks.
- Low: >4 weeks.
- Inden: tanda jadi/DP already paid.

## Retention / cross-selling logic

LOL is positioned as H1-to-H1 plus sales program. The broader retention objective is long-term Honda business through customer database utilization and cross-selling across H1/H2/H3.

RO categories shown:
- H1 to H1: repeat purchase at same dealer without own-dealer service history.
- H2 Own -> H1: purchase and service at same dealer before repeat purchase.
- H2 Others -> H1: service elsewhere and repeat purchase at the other dealer.

The CRM maturity quadrant progresses from process execution -> consistent reporting -> minimum results/growth/digital-tool consistency/NOS Gold -> continuous improvement and benchmark role-model behavior.

## Workshop case

Case: a motorcycle type declines 20% over three months while logistics holds excessive stock. Marketing must use consumer profiling, buying-cycle/migration path, PDRB and other analysis to define activity/program, delivery method, timeline and cost.

This is a direct bridge from CRM data to sales-stock problem solving.
