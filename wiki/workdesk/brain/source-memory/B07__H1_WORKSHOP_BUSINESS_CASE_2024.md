## Purpose

This deck combines prior ASSDP concepts into an end-to-end supervisory reasoning model. It begins with Demand Management, then forces the participant to translate market insight into target deployment and finally into excellent execution.

## Demand management recap

Forecasting is expressed as:

`Forecast = Pattern + Unexplained`.

Pattern / measured components include seasonality, trend/growth, competition and reasonable market-share/target assumptions. Unmeasured/contextual factors shown include economy, weather and buying power. Targeting, activity plan, promotion and stock management are the controllable response.

Demand Management combines top-down AHM and bottom-up MD planning, with Niguri/rolling plans, achievement monitoring, recommended volume/composition, stock availability and cross-function coordination.

## Target deployment chain

The workshop's core sequence is:

1. Collect market-area information.
2. Determine market share and regional growth.
3. Determine market, M/S, retail-sales target and market contribution by kabupaten.
4. Determine network coverage and strategy needed to achieve target sales.
5. Determine sales target per sales person, per series.

The reasoning explicitly asks:
- which area is growing vs Main Dealer;
- why the growth differs;
- which dealer owns the kabupaten target;
- who covers each kecamatan;
- what activity each dealer will execute;
- current sales-people capacity;
- additional SM/SC/POS/activity needed to close the gap.

## "Doctor for dealer" model

The deck contrasts raw technical work with a doctor workflow:

`history -> physical examination -> analysis/evaluation -> diagnosis -> treatment plan`.

The supervisory analogy is: do not merely carry data to dealers; become a "doctor" who turns data into diagnosis and treatment.

## Data -> Information -> Knowledge

Source definitions:

- **Data**: raw data/theory.
- **Information**: data that has been given meaning.
- **Knowledge**: information connected to a specific context.

The example shows why target growth, market share and segment purchasing-power facts only become useful after being connected to product focus, CRM, area potential and a concrete action choice.

## Excellent execution

The bridge from strategy to execution asks three concrete questions:

- Where exactly will BTL be executed?
- What product will be taken there?
- What program will be offered to the target consumer?

The purpose is **on-point target execution**, not activity for activity's sake.

## Source summary insight

The final summary states:
1. Be clear on target based on market insight: market info, CDB, product segmentation and people.
2. Calculate the right resources: people, budget, forecasting/planning method, aligned with Reshape Network.
3. Allocate people according to strength and role: Team Leader, Sales Counter, Salesman, Magang.
4. Monitor performance and adapt to market.
