## What the case demonstrates
The project is evidence of translating Basic training into dealer routines across seven themes:
- Dealer Review / Integrated Network Review
- Territory Management
- Sales Target & Forecasting
- Niguri
- PICA
- Sales & Stock Monitoring
- NOS

Examples of the transfer include monthly integrated review, dealer/kecamatan contribution monitoring, activity and salesperson productivity planning, target/forecast tools, Niguri planning, PICA with due date/evidence, daily sales/stock and old-VIN/type monitoring, and NOS visit/checklist/coaching around Moment of Truth.

## Why it matters
This is not the authority for each method; the original method sources remain authoritative. Its value is as **implementation-transfer memory**: a supervisor can convert training into dealer management routines, monitoring cadence and coaching rather than leaving the concepts as classroom material.
