## Role model
The Area SPV role spans four interacting responsibilities:
1. target and sales strategy;
2. sales/stock analysis and management;
3. customer focus;
4. evaluation and development.

## Daily operating cycle
**Planning:** area/dealer sales and activity plan, daily/weekly/monthly preparation.  
**Executing:** dealer accompaniment, dealer and fincoy coordination, activity supervision.  
**Monitoring:** market/indent/urgent stock, daily sales target, dealer activities, leasing-company achievement and daily talk.  
**Evaluating:** sales plan, activity result, dealer condition, NOS, people, program and surrounding condition.

## Dealer relationship
The source differentiates three communication modes:
- tactical/operational communication for supervision and daily issues;
- strategic communication for formal performance/growth/challenge review;
- relational communication to strengthen the partnership.

The partnership model emphasizes complementary strengths, common mission and reciprocity/fairness/trust/acceptance/communication.

## Change support
Examples include recruitment, remuneration/incentive change, benchmarking/sharing and management/dealer visits. Treat those as historical examples; the durable method is **plan -> execute -> monitor -> evaluate -> develop relationship/capability.**
