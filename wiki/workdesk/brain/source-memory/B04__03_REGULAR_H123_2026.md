# NOS 2026 — Regular H123

**Source:** `03. Checklist 2026_Final - Regular H123.xlsx`

Regular H123 is the most useful baseline model because it contains complete H1 + H23 standards without the full premium BigWing layer.

## H1 Premises
Covers network cleanliness/layout, exterior/interior, promotion, unit display, EV corner, riding test, indent board, negotiation/dealing material, customer facilities, payment, Google Business Profile, warehouse, delivery, CRM follow-up infrastructure, briefing room, MyHero, loyalty cover, FIFO.

Important current formulas/rules include:
- Warehouse capacity = `Dealer Stock Days target MD / 30 × Target Daily Sales Dealer`.
- Warehouse area = `capacity (unit) × 2.2 m²`.
- Delivery car minimum = `ROUNDUP(target sales per month / 200)`; centralized dealer-group warehouse uses group total average monthly sales with same 1:200 ratio.
- First-call follow-up maximum H+5 after unit delivery.

## H1 Process
Full customer journey and operating support: arrival/security, appointment/follow-up, needs discovery, product/after-sales explanation, Customer Apps, accessories/apparel, riding test, negotiation, purchase/credit/indent, payment, delivery, document handling, CRM analysis/distribution/monitoring, discount approval, warehouse/PDI, privacy, EV battery/charger checks.

## H1 People
Role availability, staffing ratios, training coverage, tools, appearance, CRM/PDI/warehouse/cashier/security/delivery capability. Examples: PIC CRM dedicated for H123 Wing/BigWing and for Regular H123 when sales ≥150/month; PDI Man 1 up to average sales 375/month then target sales/375, with group-centralization logic under Reshape.

## H23
Aftersales covers booking/reminder, SA reception, workshop execution, final inspection, payment, follow-up, parts/hotline order, warehouse/tools, EV low-voltage safety, people ratios/training. Examples: SA max 1,000 UE; FI max 2,000 UE; Kamek max 10 mechanics / 2,000 UE; Admin CRM H23 dedicated at ≥1,500 UE.

## Limitation
Blank audit template; formula reference syntax carries workbook-index artifacts in some references.
