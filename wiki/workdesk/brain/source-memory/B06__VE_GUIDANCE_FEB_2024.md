## What the source is doing

The deck shifts Virtual Exhibition (VE) from an event-presence mindset into a measurable lead-to-sales operating system. It reviews 2023 VE performance, MDMS/NMS integration, the platform roadmap, and a 2024 activity plan.

## Core operating model

1. Generate and capture leads from Independent and Finance Company VE events.
2. Improve contactability and lead quality before distribution/follow-up.
3. Follow pending and not-deal customers again, including customers waiting on unit/color availability.
4. Keep indent customers updated so a later SPK is reflected as deal.
5. Follow up valid contacts repeatedly at different times/days rather than treating one failed contact as final.
6. Run VE consistently and coordinate special programs with finance companies instead of simply repeating regular programs.
7. Integrate distribution, follow-up, and reporting through MDMS/NMS and maintain data quality against AHM reporting.
8. Evaluate VE not only by execution frequency but by lead quality, conversion, deal, and retail contribution.

## Historical KPI evidence

Page 3 states 2023 KPI targets:
- Success Rate = 40%.
- Contribution to RS = 0.55%.

Page 14 states 2024 activity-plan targets:
- Success Rate = Deal / Contacted = 40%.
- Contribution to RS = Deal / Retail SSU = 0.83%.
- Conversion Rate = Deal / Leads = 25%.
- VE execution = 2x/month.

These are dated targets. They must not be treated as current 2026 policy without newer authority.

## Platform development logic

Pages 9-11 show VE evolving from access/display toward interactive customer journey and integrated lead management:
- 3D product/booth and dynamic web access;
- price/credit, program and product information;
- chat/live interaction;
- H2/AHASS service information and booking;
- finance-company booth/credit simulation;
- trade-in;
- customer apps/digital assets;
- customer feedback;
- NMS & CRM API integration.

This source therefore belongs to both **customer-experience design** and **lead-conversion governance**.
