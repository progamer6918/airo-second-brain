## Define
The project frames high-aging stock (`>150 days`) and old type/variant stock as a business problem because it can create potential dead stock, dealer cash-flow pressure, lack of fincoy support and product-image issues.

The source reports a December 1, 2023 starting condition of **485 units**: 320 aging-stock units and 165 old-type units. TUNAS Group was selected as the pilot because it had the largest runout-stock contribution in the displayed comparison; the pilot stock shown is **95 units**.

## Analyze
Three cause themes are developed:
1. FIFO was not executed consistently at dealer level.
2. Salespeople focused on fast-moving units.
3. Motivation to sell aging/old variants was weak.

## Improve
The case combines process and incentive treatment:
- share/discuss the aging-stock condition with dealer management;
- dealer-funded additional salesperson incentive;
- historical case amounts: additional Rp200k for AT and Rp300k for Sport, on top of the source's regular incentive example;
- Kacab socializes the program so salespeople understand the focus.

## Control
- MD distributes daily sales/current-stock performance for old variants and high-aging units.
- Kacab reinforces achievement and guidance in morning briefing.
- Weekly coaching/counseling supports execution.

## Observed result and boundary
The source shows pilot sales increasing from **13 units in November to 26 units in December 2023** and proposes extending the approach. Preserve this as an observed historical case. Do not infer that incentive caused the full uplift or that the historical incentive amount is current.
