## Training-project contract
The 2023 Basic program uses projects as evidence that training was implemented, not just understood.

- The summary says Basic participants are required to create projects and two role-play videos as a graduation condition.
- Stage 1 specifically asks for **one project + two videos**.
- The project must explain **before vs after training**, supported by data/photo/video or other evidence.
- It must apply material taught in ASSDP; the instruction asks for multiple application points rather than a single theory mention.
- The participant's supervisor independently scores the project presentation.
- Assessment looks at implementation process, correctness of applying training material, and field result.

The source is training governance from 2023. Its enduring semantic value is the principle: **learning is only considered transferred when it is applied, evidenced and reviewed.**
