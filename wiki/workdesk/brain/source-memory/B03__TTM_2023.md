## What TTM is

The source defines Territory Management as a work tool for managing an outlet's area **effectively and efficiently to achieve sales and market-share targets in each part of its coverage area**. (slide 2)

It exists because dealers may not focus on their own areas, activities may not be systematically planned/monitored, horizontal area targets and vertical dealer targets may not align, and salesperson productivity targets may lack a clear basis. (slide 2)

## The three-part operating system

1. **Mapping Ring** — define dealer coverage and convert geography into target responsibility.
2. **Activities & Scheduling** — plan marketing activity and salesperson work against the territory target.
3. **Review & Monitoring** — compare target vs execution/results and coach/correct. (slides 3, 13–27)

## Horizontal + vertical target logic

TTM explicitly combines:

- **horizontal target** — target by area/kecamatan;
- **vertical target** — target by dealer.

Main Dealer role includes defining market/area targets and monitoring marketing/review. Dealer role includes defining salesperson targets and planning/executing/monitoring activities. (slides 2–4)

This is important: a dealer target is not enough if no one can explain **which kecamatan/ring should produce it and what activity/sales-force capacity will deliver it**.

## Mapping Ring

The 2023 example uses location, Ring 1, Ring 2, Ring 3 and Others with illustrative distance/contribution weights. The deck explicitly says ring distance should be adjusted to local area conditions. (slide 7)

The worked example then:
- maps each dealer to a ring by kecamatan;
- derives an ideal contribution split;
- allocates the next-year area target across dealers;
- compares with prior-year actual;
- adjusts targets while considering network capability/development and the minimum MD target from AHM. (slide 12)

Therefore **ring is a planning responsibility model**, not just a map color.

## Activities & sales force

The source links territory targets to dealer activity and daily sales by person. Main Dealer monitors activity and productivity; Dealer plans activity and salesperson composition, then monitors/reviews productivity. (slides 14–16)

## Review logic

Review asks three questions:

1. Did planned marketing activity cover the dealer's target by kecamatan?
2. Did implementation match the plan/target?
3. Did activity results achieve the expected target? (slides 17–19)

The TTM review form then compares target and actual sales/contribution by dealer × kecamatan, shows gap from the Dealer POV, and examines the kecamatan from the MD POV to see which dealers occupy which rings and how they perform. (slides 20–23)

The output focuses attention on **location and Ring 1**, marketing activity in those priority territories, and salesperson productivity against the mapped activity. (slides 24–25)

## Diagnostic rules

- Dealer total target achieved but Location/Ring1 weak → coverage quality can still be unhealthy; check whether sales are being sourced from outer areas or other dealers' territory.
- Area target weak with adequate activity count → check activity quality/productivity and salesperson deployment, not just add more events.
- Ring target gap → compare target contribution, actual contribution, activity coverage and dealer capability before changing territory ownership.
- "Violation" or cross-coverage should be investigated from both Dealer POV and MD POV; the deck deliberately provides both views.
