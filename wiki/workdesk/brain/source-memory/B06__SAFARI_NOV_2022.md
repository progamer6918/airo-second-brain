## Event concept

SAFARI is described as a large FIFGROUP virtual exhibition running nationally, with multiple FIFGROUP brands/business partners. The reviewed schedule was 24 Nov-5 Dec 2022.

## Customer journey

The deck shows:

`landing -> select domicile -> welcome video -> register/login -> 360 exhibition hall -> Main Dealer booth / finance-company booths / other booths -> Main Dealer VE / catalog / form order / content`.

The direct-to-MD path is designed to reduce navigation friction for invited customers.

## FIFASTRA / Honda lead flow

1. Customer enters the MD booth in SAFARI.
2. Customer clicks into the MD VE.
3. Customer registers and enters the required promo code.
4. Customer interested in a Honda unit submits a lead form.
5. MD follows the lead and records it in the agreed reporting/MonitorMu flow.
6. MD distributes leads to dealer.
7. When deal is confirmed, customer data is passed to FIFGROUP for credit processing where applicable.
8. Finance-company branch follows and processes credit under the Promo ID.

## Database activation

The source shows a controlled blast process:
- FIFGROUP prepares RO database according to required parameters;
- gives it to designated MD PIC;
- MD blasts according to event needs each day.

This is not just media buying. It is a coordinated customer-data activation process.

## Operational controls

Page 14 states, among other controls:
- link open 24h; service response window shown as 08:00-21:00 WIB in this historical event;
- leads after 21:00 should be followed the next day;
- campaign blast recommended heavily in the first three days rather than evenly spreading low volume;
- dealer team must understand flow, promo and product;
- leads captured at MD and assigned/distributed with fairness;
- daily lead and follow-up status update expected, highlighted around 10:00 WIB in this event;
- independent MD/dealer campaigns can support the collaboration;
- low-digital-literacy prospects can still be assisted through the valid VE flow;
- lead treatment follows AHM rules.

These are event-specific 2022 rules, not current universal SLA.
