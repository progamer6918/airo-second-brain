## Unique content retained
### Ambidextrous professional
The opening block frames ambidexterity as the ability to operate across two different worlds: different demands, behaviors and tensions. It connects ambidextrous leadership/selling to adaptability while retaining useful consistency. A visible paradox pair in the source is **active listening vs persuasive communication** and **adaptability vs consistency**. The practical implication is not to choose one pole permanently, but to know which behavior the situation requires.

### Visual thinking / presentation design
The final block introduces Dan Roam-style visual thinking:
- identify the meeting type: information delivery, decision making, problem solving, new idea, or sales pitch;
- use a visual decoder to turn ideas into answerable dimensions;
- structure a 10-page pitch storyline moving from title/common ground/problem to emotional win, rejection of false hope, an audacious but plausible alternative, proof of feasibility, call to action, early benefits and a long-term win.
