# Source Dossier - Business Case ASSDP Workbook

**Source:** `Business Case ASSDP (1).xlsx`  
**Sheets:** Guide, Market, Forecasting, Target per Tipe, Target per Dealer, Activity.

## Guide

The workbook tells participants to:
1. use any relevant market; Jakarta is only an example;
2. use the theories explained in training;
3. focus on strategy and new ideas, not numbers alone;
4. estimate budget needed and potential profit;
5. build a simple PPT covering market material, target-setting reasoning, budget/profit and the new strategy.

## Forecasting sheet

Contains historical/example 2024 ATH market, Honda and M/S by Jakarta/Tangerang area for Jan-Jun. It demonstrates that market, Honda volume and market share should be read together before planning future months.

## Target per Tipe

The layout runs Jun-Dec 2024 and expects, per type:

`Sales actual -> Daily Sales -> Target -> Daily Sales -> Required Distribution -> Total Stock -> Market Stock Days`.

The sample workbook has **23** in the working-day input and formulas such as `Daily Sales = Actual Sales / 23`. This is an exercise input, not a universal 23-day rule.

Most future target/distribution/stock cells are intentionally blank for participant work.

## Target per Dealer

Instructions assume five dealers per area and ask the participant to design the target split and state assumptions. This is deliberately open-ended; the workbook does not contain a hidden authoritative allocation.

## Activity

Jul-Dec activity plan by area with KPI column. Again, blank by design to force strategy creation.

## Decision meaning

The workbook is not a calculator that gives the answer. It is a **business-case scaffold** connecting market diagnosis, forecast/target, product mix, dealer allocation, activity, KPI, budget and profit.
