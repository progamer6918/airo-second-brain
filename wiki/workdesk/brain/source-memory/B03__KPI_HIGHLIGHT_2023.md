## Why this source matters

The deck is a **Sales Division system map**. It links division goals to a set of mutually dependent KPI families rather than treating retail sales or market share as standalone outcomes.

The background problems are: weak understanding of RS/MS/winning area, lost people/network potential, weak customer/company engagement, underused dealer groups, declining cash composition affecting dealer sustainability, and rising dealer OPEX. The response is situational analysis across the whole commercial system. (slide 4)

## KPI reading loop

The source's analysis flow is:

**Actual YTD in year Y → target for Y+1 → monthly actual/achievement in Y+1 → YTD actual/achievement in Y+1.** (slides 5–6)

The reusable principle is to read a KPI in time context: baseline, target, monthly path and cumulative achievement. A single month or a single percentage is not enough.

## Division-goal architecture

### 1. RS, MS & Winning Area
Read retail-sales achievement by total/segment/type-series, market-share condition by total/9-segment/3-segment, and winning area by total/segment. Goal: not merely grow total sales, but win by type/segment/area. (slide 9)

### 2. People & Network Development
People: quantity of sales roles, productivity/competency development, sales tools, knowledge/spirit.  
Network: dealer/POS footprint, channel productivity, dealer mapping, Territory Management, cross coverage and NOS. (slide 10)

### 3. CRM & CSL
CRM includes RO sales, RO by design, cross selling and customer-data quality. CSL is linked to improvement of top issues by function/MD and focus items by dealer layer. (slide 11)

### 4. ATL, BTL & Group Customer
The source reads relationship-building not only by spend but by footprint/frequency/productivity/cost/engagement and Group Customer team/mapping/sales/contribution/gathering. (slide 12)

### 5. Smart Network / Digitalization
Examples include prospect-to-deal via NMS, consistency of NMS utilization, CE apps adoption and completeness of data from all sources through conversion to deal. (slide 13)

### 6. Dealer Group
Read number/configuration of dealer groups, sales by group, contribution to total sales and MD review. (slide 14)

### 7. Cash / Credit sustainability
Read cash volume/composition and credit volume/composition plus sales/contribution by major finance companies. The source frames mix as a dealer-sustainability issue, not merely a financing statistic. (slide 15)

## Cross-source implication

This deck is best used as an **index of diagnostic branches**. When RS/MS is weak, the next question is not automatically "make a promo". The system invites branching into people, network/territory, customer, activity, digital funnel, group structure and financing sustainability.
