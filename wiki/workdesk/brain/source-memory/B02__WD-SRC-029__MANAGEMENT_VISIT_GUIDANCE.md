# WD-SRC-029 — Management Visit Guidance

## Kenapa sumber ini berguna di WorkDesk

Judulnya visit guidance, tetapi semantic value terbesar untuk WorkDesk adalah **checklist kelengkapan managerial diagnosis**: market, customer, sales, people, product, program, competitor, NOS, after-sales dan forward strategy. Ia tidak boleh dipakai sebagai kewajiban bahwa setiap Dealer Review harus menanyakan semua pertanyaan setiap saat.

## Preparation model

Slide 2 memberikan ideal preparation timeline historis D+3/D+5/D+7/D+10 untuk dealer list, schedule/route, profile/comparison, Q&A dan presentation. Ini workflow visit 2023, bukan current SLA kecuali dikonfirmasi lagi.

Dealer Profile (slides 7–9) mencakup:

- dealer information;
- sales performance;
- manpower;
- sales composition;
- pricing comparison;
- BTL activity;
- fincoy condition.

## Management Q&A lens

Slides 11–24 menyediakan diagnostic question bank.

### Market
- current market + forward outlook;
- M vs M-1, Y vs Y-1, before/after seasonal events;
- economic/commodity/infrastructure drivers;
- nearby market potential and customer occupation;
- segment contribution and shifting.

### Customer
- walk-in customer profile;
- occupation, age, intended user, cash/credit;
- customer mix at showroom/activity.

### Sales performance
- current sales and forecast;
- daily sales week-to-week;
- target achievement;
- segment shifting and why;
- focus type;
- stock/indent and indent duration;
- strategy/activity next 2–3 months.

### Product and competitor
- acceptance against direct competitor by engine/model/acceleration/features/dimension/riding experience/color;
- new product acceptance;
- competitor dealer/location, sales movement, activity/program, readiness/indent and bestseller.

### Program / pricing
- compare actual printed PL and actual customer payment for focus types;
- evaluate whether AHM/MD/dealer initiatives were actually executed;
- test whether incentive/direct gift/discount/activity affected sales rather than assuming effectiveness.

### People / activity / network
- active SM/SC and incentive/target deployment;
- focused activity by segment;
- showroom/NOS readiness;
- after-sales Unit Entry/KPB/return behavior as complementary dealer-health context.

## What-good-analysis behavior extracted

Source repeatedly asks comparison questions rather than snapshots:

- M vs M-1;
- week vs week;
- before vs after event;
- Y vs Y-1;
- target vs actual;
- Honda vs competitor;
- plan vs actual program/activity.

WorkDesk synthesis: **Dealer Review should explain movement, not describe a static dashboard.**

## Sensitive external communication guard

Slide 26 contains specific internal Do/Don't items for external/partner visits. Those details are context-sensitive and potentially sensitive. Public WorkDesk should only preserve this safe rule:

> Before an external/partner visit, separate internal diagnostic truth from information approved for external communication. Use the current visit brief/policy as authority; do not infer that an old 2023 Do/Don't remains current.
