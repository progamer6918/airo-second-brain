## Market sensing before strategy
The sharing starts from district-level market structure, economic drivers, seasonal/religious events, tourism/agriculture/service cycles and market-share differences by area/segment. The reusable principle is to make sales strategy responsive to **local demand drivers**, not only province-level history.

## 4P strategy
The Main Dealer strategy is organized around Product, Price, Place and Promotion. Examples include increasing share in weak areas/pareto segments, adapting sales programs to local conditions, strengthening credit/GC contribution, adapting supply-demand, improving brand recognition, collaborating AHM-MD-Dealer and leveraging fincoy strength.

## Control rhythm
- Weekly check per area: record why sales moves differently by area.
- Weekly check per dealer: target vs actual determines whether the dealer is on track, can be pushed, or needs support.
- Network readiness includes remuneration, recruitment support, salesperson development/training and contest mechanisms.

## Execution portfolio
The historical activities cover product-specific branding, community events, roadshows/expos, digital/KOL exposure, CRM/lead treatment, fincoy programs, POS competition, village/market activation, canvassing, institutional/GC visits and ATL/BTL.
