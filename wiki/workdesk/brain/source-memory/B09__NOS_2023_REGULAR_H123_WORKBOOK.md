## Workbook architecture
The 2023 Regular H123 audit template separates:
- H1 Premises
- H1 People
- H1 Process
- H23 Premises
- H23 People
- H23 Process
- grading criteria
- consolidated Result Summary

The grading sheet defines:
- Bronze: 0-59.9%
- Silver: 60-69.9%
- Gold: 70-89.9%
- Platinum: 90-100%

The Result Summary brings component audit results together with Mandatory Item status before the final result. Mandatory markers appear in the operational sheets (for example `P`, `G, P`, `S, G, P`).

## Template/formula behavior
The workbook is an **empty audit template**. Inspection found `#DIV/0!` in percentage/result cells because required input counts are blank/zero. These are not evidence that a dealer failed NOS; they are empty-template calculation effects.

Examples of formula patterns inspected include ratios of marked score to possible score and summary links from component sheets into Result Summary.

The 2023 workbook remains useful for lineage: it already operationalized H1/H23 Premises-People-Process, mandatory items, audit scoring and consolidated result before the later Reshape/digital/network-tier expansion.
