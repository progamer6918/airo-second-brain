## What NOS is

The source defines NOS as standardization of service across the network at every **Moment of Truth (MOT)** so customers receive a consistent service experience wherever they interact with the network. (slide 2)

The objective evolves beyond checklist compliance: create consistent showroom/service experience, adapt to changing market/customer expectations, maintain network sustainability, make NOS a network culture, update customer journey with digital transformation and streamline the process. (slide 3)

## Three components

NOS organizes service execution around:

- **Premises** — the physical environment;
- **People** — frontline/role capability and behavior;
- **Process** — how service is executed through the journey. (slide 6)

The service blueprint/customer journeys connect these dimensions to the actual customer path. The 2023 H1 journey includes steps from appointment/arrival and showroom/dealing through unit selection, documentation/payment, handover, follow-up and registration; the H23 journey follows service booking/reminder, arrival, consultation, work approval, waiting, final confirmation/payment and follow-up. (slides 7–9)

## Assessment system

NOS validation combines internal and external lenses.

### Internal audit by MD

Preparation includes communication, audit period/schedule, cross-function audit team and forms/evidence tools. Assessment categories in the 2023 source are Exist Good, Exist Not Good, Not Exist and N/A, with different point logic across Premises/Process/People. (slide 11)

During audit:
- observe customer experience against indicators;
- write clear notes to support later PICA;
- audit premises via observation/interview;
- process via observation/roleplay/interview;
- people via job-role/Kacab/MD training interviews;
- capture photo/video evidence;
- refer to the NOS guidance when ambiguous;
- score objectively. (slide 12)

### Mandatory vs non-mandatory

The source distinguishes mandatory items that constrain network status from non-mandatory items with longer improvement windows. 2023 correction windows shown are premises/process two weeks, people one month, and non-mandatory three–six months. These time windows are **historical** until current 2026 authority confirms. (slide 12)

### Mystery Shopping & Calling

External assessment captures indicators that require a real customer interaction and complements MD audit. The shopper must understand dealer process, be objective, observant, have integrity and not be recognized by frontline staff. A scenario and checklist are prepared before execution. (slide 13)

## Diagnostic meaning

NOS is not merely a score. Audit evidence should become **service-gap diagnosis → PICA → recheck**. A mandatory miss can dominate the resulting network status, so the diagnostic should identify whether the weakest link is premises, process or people and whether it is systemic or isolated.
