## Program context

The 2-4 Sep 2024 Intermediate program includes Practical Problem Solving, Negotiation, Customer Satisfaction/Customer Focus, Design Thinking, H1 workshop/case and CRM workshop. This confirms the intended integration: market/sales supervisors are expected to combine analytical, customer, execution and interpersonal capabilities rather than use them as isolated modules.

## Final-project standard

Required topic:

**"Breakthrough Strategy & Activity Winning Market - AT High"** in the participant's own area.

Required content:
1. Market Insight / background.
2. Breakdown Target / project goals.
3. Excellent Execution / monitoring and implementation.
4. CRM linkage earns plus points.

Implementation requirement in the historical letter:
- applied in the participant's work area;
- minimum four dealers;
- evidence of field monitoring;
- before-vs-after result;
- presentation quality/public speaking included in assessment.

This is important because ASSDP Intermediate's definition of competence is **applied integration**, not theory recall.

Historical administrative details such as Jan 2025 submission deadline, Feb-Mar 2025 presentation timing and Rp4,000,000 training-cost recovery are preserved only as historical context.
