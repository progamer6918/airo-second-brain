## Expected capability triad - slide 2

### Business Sense
SPV Area is expected to:
- determine an end-to-end strategy across planning, targeting, monitoring and evaluation;
- set accurate plan/target per dealer using market-condition analysis;
- perform deep performance analysis;
- drive dealers to execute guidance.

### Strategic & Advanced Leadership
SPV should:
- monitor each activity process against guidance;
- evaluate performance/result strongly;
- determine corrective action strategy (PICA) that is sharp and creative.

### Innovation
SPV should:
- adapt to changing customer preferences;
- maximize digitalization across activities;
- create new activity-plan innovations, deploy them to dealers, and monitor dealer execution.

## PDCA responsibility - slide 3

Plan:
- short-to-medium strategy and operational activity plan to reach function target.

Do:
- cross-functional coordination;
- analyze area potential and competitor movement;
- coordinate external parties where required.

Check:
- control and monitor implementation/achievement;
- evaluate performance and readiness/development of process and people.

Action:
- develop growth strategy;
- build internal development programs / talent readiness.

## Minimum dealer review - slide 4

Dealer Review should not be sales-only. Minimum lenses:

1. **Sales Performance** - target/achievement, previous-period comparison, stock days/type, cash-credit and fincoy approval/reject/cancel, hyperlocal market/forecast, RO/ROBD, migration/buying-cycle context.
2. **Manpower** - target vs actual people by layer, people trend, salespeople productivity, mapping and recovery.
3. **Sales Activities** - activity points, BTL point/theme, target vs actual prospects/sales/activity, contribution and source of sales.
4. **Development** - DCSL/NOS/INS missed items, TTM, POS, leads workload/conversion.
5. **Sales Composition** - Walk-in vs Salesman, detail sources and trends.

## Activity formulation - slides 5-6

SPV must combine:
`local positive/negative issues + historical sales trend + mapping area + activity need + frequency + sales target + sales-force contribution/headcount/productivity`.

## Deep monitoring - slide 7

Dashboard hierarchy includes:
- Kabupaten performance;
- Dealer performance;
- Stock;
- Fincoy;
- Series;
- Daily sales trend.

## Summary role - slide 9

The strongest mental model:

1. **SPV Area as Connector** - ensure Dealer executes Main Dealer guidance/strategy to achieve target.
2. **SPV Area as Dealer Consultant** - understand dealer condition/hot issue, identify improvement and decide how to support dealer to achieve more.

This means a good SPV is not a courier of instruction. The SPV must translate, diagnose, influence and support execution.
