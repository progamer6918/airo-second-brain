# Applied Case - AT High Merangin Brand Awareness

## Situation
YTD Sep 2024 Honda volume grew, but competitor/market grew faster. Honda AT High share in Merangin was 28.5%, while Yamaha was 71.0%.

## Insight
The case combines economic drivers, commodity/harvest context, infrastructure/credit, low brand awareness in remote areas and kecamatan-level potential.

## Target
Historical Oct-Nov-Dec M/S targets: 30%, 32.5%, 35%. Retail targets: 94, 84, 129. Four focus dealers.

## Action
- Touring & Roadshow Festival.
- ATL & BTL brand exposure.
- CSR/community activation.

## Monitoring
Weekly field activity; monthly sales/M/S and dealer/PIC effectiveness.

## Reported result
- M/S: 30.6% Oct, 44.6% Nov, 40.6% Dec.
- Retail actual: 95, 114, 138.
- Touring: 100 participants, 5 direct units, 10 hot prospects.
- Walk-in reportedly +30%.
- 3 CSR activities.

## What to learn
The reusable lesson is the reasoning chain, **not** the historical target values or the assumption that the same activities will create the same effect elsewhere.
