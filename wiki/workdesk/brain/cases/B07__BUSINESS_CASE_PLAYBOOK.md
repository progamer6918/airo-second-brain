# Business Case Playbook

## Minimum structure

### 1. Market Insight
What changed? Where? Why? Which customer/area/product?

### 2. Target Breakdown
Market -> M/S -> retail -> dealer -> sales people -> type/series.

### 3. Strategy
What is new/different and why should it work?

### 4. Execution
Place, product, target customer, program, people, timeline, KPI.

### 5. Economics
Budget/cost and expected profit/benefit. Separate assumption from source fact.

### 6. Monitoring
Weekly execution signal + monthly business outcome.

### 7. Result
Before/after, explain what evidence supports the causal story and what remains uncertain.
