## Source-derived module — Market Insight To Excellent Execution
_Reconstruction module: `B07` / `MARKET_INSIGHT_TO_EXCELLENT_EXECUTION.md`_

## The intermediate-level supervisory chain

`market/customer insight -> target logic -> network/dealer/people deployment -> exact activity/program/product -> monitoring -> adaptation`.

### Market insight
Use market size, M/S, Honda sales, economic drivers, customer/CDB, product segmentation and people capacity.

### Target
Break down:
`MD/area -> kabupaten -> dealer -> sales people -> series/type`.

A target is incomplete until ownership and coverage are clear.

### Resource
Calculate people, budget and planning/forecasting needs. Allocate roles according to strengths and role requirements.

### Excellent execution
Specify:
- exact place;
- exact product;
- exact customer;
- exact program;
- exact PIC;
- exact timing;
- monitoring metric.

### Adaptation
Monitor performance and market response; change execution when evidence changes.

## "Doctor for dealer" rule

Do not carry raw data into a dealer meeting and call it analysis. Convert data to information, connect it to context, diagnose, and propose treatment.

## Source-derived module — Business Case Engineering
_Reconstruction module: `B07` / `BUSINESS_CASE_ENGINEERING.md`_

## Required logic

The combined sources define a business case as more than an Excel forecast:

1. **Market Insight** - why the problem/opportunity exists.
2. **Breakdown Target** - what result is required and how it decomposes.
3. **Excellent Execution** - what strategy/activity will be executed and monitored.
4. **Economics** - budget/cost and expected profit or benefit.
5. **Evidence** - before/after and field-monitoring proof.
6. **Customer/CRM linkage** - where relevant, connect the strategy to actual customer data/needs/leads.

## Anti-patterns

- numbers with no new strategy;
- activity list with no market problem;
- target with no ownership/coverage;
- budget with no expected benefit;
- "customer focus" with no CDB/need evidence;
- before/after without explaining causal mechanism;
- one-dealer anecdote presented as area strategy.

## Training-case workbook role

The workbook intentionally leaves future targets/activity blank. It is a thinking scaffold, not a hidden answer key.

### B07 — AT HIGH MERANGIN CASE

## Situation
YTD Sep 2024 Honda volume grew, but competitor/market grew faster. Honda AT High share in Merangin was 28.5%, while Yamaha was 71.0%.

## Insight
The case combines economic drivers, commodity/harvest context, infrastructure/credit, low brand awareness in remote areas and kecamatan-level potential.

## Target
Historical Oct-Nov-Dec M/S targets: 30%, 32.5%, 35%. Retail targets: 94, 84, 129. Four focus dealers.

## Action
- Touring & Roadshow Festival.
- ATL & BTL brand exposure.
- CSR/community activation.

## Monitoring
Weekly field activity; monthly sales/M/S and dealer/PIC effectiveness.

## Reported result
- M/S: 30.6% Oct, 44.6% Nov, 40.6% Dec.
- Retail actual: 95, 114, 138.
- Touring: 100 participants, 5 direct units, 10 hot prospects.
- Walk-in reportedly +30%.
- 3 CSR activities.

## What to learn
The reusable lesson is the reasoning chain, **not** the historical target values or the assumption that the same activities will create the same effect elsewhere.

### B07 — BUSINESS CASE PLAYBOOK

## Minimum structure

### 1. Market Insight
What changed? Where? Why? Which customer/area/product?

### 2. Target Breakdown
Market -> M/S -> retail -> dealer -> sales people -> type/series.

### 3. Strategy
What is new/different and why should it work?

### 4. Execution
Place, product, target customer, program, people, timeline, KPI.

### 5. Economics
Budget/cost and expected profit/benefit. Separate assumption from source fact.

### 6. Monitoring
Weekly execution signal + monthly business outcome.

### 7. Result
Before/after, explain what evidence supports the causal story and what remains uncertain.

### B08 — PENETRASI MERANGIN CASE

## Problem
Merangin punya volume market besar tetapi FY2025 Honda M/S **79.64%**, di bawah Jambi **81.09%**.

## Insight
Source case menemukan:
- sekitar 59% customer base = petani;
- 86.99% Sales Counter source = walk-in;
- canvassing kuat (58.02% pada salesman source);
- AHASS waiting-room contribution baru 1.11%.

Hipotesis: Honda terlalu menunggu customer datang dan belum cukup hadir di komunitas/tempat keputusan terbentuk.

## Historical target Q1
- Jan 80.0%;
- Feb 80.5%;
- Mar 81.0%.

## Three-pillar response

1. **Warung Presence** - hyper-local brand/contact point.
2. **Community Engagement** - structured territory canvassing + community participation.
3. **AHASS Strike** - waiting-room prospect activation and follow-up.

## Monitoring
Daily dealer/salesperson dashboard + monthly market/dealer/activity review.

## Reported result
Source reports Jan M/S 82.85% and Feb 80.40%, both above the staged target. Dealer-sales totals also beat Jan/Feb target but March dealer sales were below March target.

## Caution
The page labels “aggregate Q1 80.8%” even though March market actual is shown TBD. Treat 80.8% as source-reported narrative with timing ambiguity, not a fully reconciled closed-Q1 market fact.

## What to copy
Copy the reasoning:
`paradox -> evidence -> cause -> intervention mapped to cause -> control`.

Do **not** copy the historical target or assume warung/community/AHASS will always create the same outcome.

### B09 — AGING STOCK RUNOUT CASE

## Situasi
Historical Owner project 2023: aging stock >150 days + old variants menumpuk.

## Diagnosis
- FIFO tidak konsisten;
- salesperson mengejar fast-moving unit;
- motivasi runout rendah.

## Treatment
Dealer pilot + program incentive historical + socialization + daily sales/stock visibility + morning briefing + weekly coaching.

## Result source
Pilot sales old/aging units shown: 13 units in Nov -> 26 in Dec 2023.

## Jangan copy mentah
Jangan pakai nominal insentif historis sebagai program sekarang dan jangan menganggap 2x sales pasti disebabkan incentive. Gunakan case ini sebagai pola **process + motivation + control**, lalu cek current stock, margin, program dan dealer economics.
