# Business Sensing Market Intelligence

> **Status:** canonical persistence candidate derived only from reconstructed source material. Historical examples remain historical; unresolved source conflicts remain unresolved.

## Canonical scope
- economic / local drivers
- market size/share/segment/area
- competitor
- customer profile and needs
- territory/ring/coverage

## Canonical diagnostic guardrails
- **Volume growth does not imply market-share growth.** Market share is relative to the same-period total-market denominator; competitor growth and/or denominator growth can make volume rise while M/S falls, or volume fall while M/S rises.
- Never explain M/S movement without checking the denominator and competitor movement when those data are available.

- Start from the business question, then localize the problem before diagnosing.
- Resolve requested date/currentness before applying dated standards, thresholds, program rules, or examples.

## Source-derived module — Market Insight To Excellent Execution
_Reconstruction module: `B07` / `MARKET_INSIGHT_TO_EXCELLENT_EXECUTION.md`_

## The intermediate-level supervisory chain

`market/customer insight -> target logic -> network/dealer/people deployment -> exact activity/program/product -> monitoring -> adaptation`.

### Market insight
Use market size, M/S, Honda sales, economic drivers, customer/CDB, product segmentation and people capacity.

### Target
Break down:
`MD/area -> kabupaten -> dealer -> sales people -> series/type`.

A target is incomplete until ownership and coverage are clear.

### Resource
Calculate people, budget and planning/forecasting needs. Allocate roles according to strengths and role requirements.

### Excellent execution
Specify:
- exact place;
- exact product;
- exact customer;
- exact program;
- exact PIC;
- exact timing;
- monitoring metric.

### Adaptation
Monitor performance and market response; change execution when evidence changes.

## "Doctor for dealer" rule

Do not carry raw data into a dealer meeting and call it analysis. Convert data to information, connect it to context, diagnose, and propose treatment.

## Source-derived module — Nd Plan And Area Review Model
_Reconstruction module: `B05` / `ND_PLAN_AND_AREA_REVIEW_MODEL.md`_

## Area review is not one-dimensional

The ND Plan review template forces area review across macro-economy, market, network, people, service, digital, and sustainability.

## Required evidence stack

1. **PDRB / market driven**: top economic sectors YoY, area pareto, area concern.
2. **PDRB vs CDB comparison**: checks whether actual customer database is aligned with economic-driver sectors.
3. **Quarterly market information**: positive/negative sector facts by quarter.
4. **Business milestone**: H1/H2/H3/HC3/Development KPIs across 2019-2026/YTD.
5. **Position audit by area**: latest YTD vs prior YTD.
6. **Functional evaluations**:
   - H1: market share, 9 segment, cash/credit, EV, competitor movement.
   - H2: UE, SPR, revenue/month, sales ability, customer movement, coverage, LCR.
   - H3: Sales In/Out total/HGP/AHM Oil/Acc/Apparel and by area/product.
   - HC3: CSL, CRM retention, leads management.
   - Digitalization: NMS H1/H2, CE Apps, AstraPay, feature usage.
7. **Mapping area sustainability**: Strong/Optimize/Develop/Weak.
8. **Market growth x contribution quadrant**: pick priority review areas.
9. **Area deep review**: integrated performance, mapping network, mapping ring, TTM, people productivity, PICA.

## Rule for using examples

Dummy/sample numbers in the deck should train the analysis flow, not become business facts.

## Source-derived module — Ttm To Nd Plan To Best Cross Source Synthesis
_Reconstruction module: `B05` / `TTM_TO_ND_PLAN_TO_BEST_CROSS_SOURCE_SYNTHESIS.md`_

## The integrated flow

Batch03 introduced TTM as territory mapping and activity planning. Batch05 makes the integration explicit:

1. AHM sets/breaks down Sales, M/S, UE, and SPR targets.
2. MD breaks down targets by area, network, POS, kabupaten, and kecamatan.
3. Historical area/network/POS data informs calculation of Unit and UE target by mapping ring.
4. MD finalizes coverage strategy and final target Sales/UE.
5. Network executes and monitors activity weekly/monthly.
6. MD reviews TTM result and PICA quarterly.
7. ND Plan review incorporates TTM results into area/network strategy.
8. BEST monthly meetings monitor execution discipline, accuracy, and follow-up.

## Diagnostic example

If Market Share drops in an area, the WorkDesk question sequence becomes:

- Is the area a pareto/concern area by contribution/growth?
- Does PDRB/CDB show a customer segment mismatch?
- Is Honda network/POS/AHASS coverage enough in the relevant ring/kecamatan?
- Did network/POS execute planned activity and submit accurate actual BTL?
- Are salespeople/mechanics productive enough?
- Did leads/register/CRM/NMS conversion fail?
- Is NOS/INS/sustainability blocking customer experience?
- Does reshape/sharing function help with cost/productivity/process?
- What PICA is specific, measurable, and due-dated?

## Source-derived module — Network Territory Sustainability Operating System
_Reconstruction module: `B03` / `NETWORK_TERRITORY_SUSTAINABILITY_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`_

This synthesis compiles the six Batch03 decks into one reasoning model. It does **not** make 2023 thresholds current.

## The system

**KPI Highlight** provides the top-level commercial outcome map: RS, MS, winning area, people/network, CRM/CSL, activities, digitalization, dealer groups and financing sustainability.

**Channel Management** answers: *do we have the right physical network coverage and are new/replacement/upgrade channels being delivered on time and economically?*

**Territory Management (TTM)** answers: *given the network, which area is each outlet responsible for, what should each territory contribute, and are activities/salespeople deployed to produce it?*

**NOS** answers: *does the network deliver a consistent customer experience through premises, people and process?*

**My Hero** is a people-enablement mechanism: distribute knowledge/tools, test knowledge, expose utilization/performance to Kacab, and focus coaching.

**INS** wraps the network into sustainability: quantitative performance/potential + qualitative owner/business/financial/regeneration risk + NOS + customer satisfaction + digital capability.

## A practical diagnostic sequence

When an area/dealer underperforms, do not jump directly to promotion.

1. **Outcome:** Which RS/MS/winning-area metric is weak? Is the issue total, segment, type or territory?
2. **Coverage:** Is the channel footprint adequate? Are new/replacement/upgrade/POS plans late or mismatched to market need?
3. **Territory:** Is target ownership by kecamatan/ring clear? Is the dealer winning Location/Ring1? Is cross-coverage masking weakness?
4. **Activity & people:** Is enough activity happening in the right territory, and is salesperson productivity adequate?
5. **Service:** Does NOS reveal premises/process/people breakdown that can reduce conversion, satisfaction or loyalty?
6. **Enablement:** Are salespeople actually using knowledge/recommendation tools, and do quiz/qualification results show capability gaps?
7. **Sustainability:** Is the dealer commercially weak because of deeper owner, succession, financial, customer or digital capability issues?
8. **Corrective loop:** turn evidence into PICA, monitor recovery, and recheck the relevant KPI family.

## Important tensions

- **More channels can reduce sustainability** if volume/economics are insufficient.
- **More activity can be wasteful** if placed outside priority territory or if salesperson productivity is weak.
- **High sales can coexist with fragile sustainability** when owner succession, cash flow, customer experience or process capability is weak.
- **High app login is not the goal**; capability and execution are.
- **NOS score is not the end state**; the objective is consistent service and continuous improvement.

## Source-derived module — Strategy To Execution And Breakthrough
_Reconstruction module: `B08` / `STRATEGY_TO_EXECUTION_AND_BREAKTHROUGH.md`_

## ASSDP Advance standard

The final-project requirement defines the expected supervisory product:

**Breakthrough Integrated Strategy & Activity to Develop Weak Area into a Strength Area.**

This means “strategy” is not a PowerPoint idea. It must integrate:

`Demand/Market -> Marketing Strategy -> Target -> Execution -> Monitoring -> Evaluation -> Corrective Action -> Next Strategy`.

## Weak-area breakthrough logic

1. **Prove the weak area** - gap, denominator, contribution and benchmark.
2. **Find why it matters** - size/potential, strategic contribution, customer/economic context.
3. **Break the gap down** - area/product/dealer/people/activity/customer/process.
4. **Test root causes** - evidence over narrative.
5. **Set target** - quantified, staged, owned and tied to market assumptions.
6. **Design integrated response** - each intervention should map to a cause.
7. **Use channel/network/digitalization** - not just marketing activity.
8. **Monitor leading + lagging indicators**.
9. **Evaluate effectiveness** - activity completed is not activity effective.
10. **Standardize / scale / redesign** based on evidence.

## PENETRASI example

The Merangin case demonstrates this pattern:
- high-volume market but share below province;
- farmer/community decision context;
- sales dependence on walk-in;
- canvassing + AHASS opportunity;
- three interventions mapped to three root causes;
- daily sales monitoring + monthly market/activity review.

Do not copy the interventions mechanically. Copy the **cause-to-intervention architecture**.

## Breakthrough gate

A proposal is not “breakthrough” merely because it is new. It needs:
- meaningful gap/opportunity;
- different mechanism from business-as-usual;
- explicit hypothesis;
- measurable target;
- evidence during execution;
- control/learning loop.

## Source-derived module — Advanced Area Supervisor Operating System
_Reconstruction module: `B08` / `ADVANCED_AREA_SUPERVISOR_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`_

## Core identity

An advanced Area SPV is simultaneously:

- **business sensor** - understands market, dealer, customer, competitor and performance evidence;
- **strategy translator** - turns MD intent into area/dealer-specific target and action;
- **connector** - makes cross-function and MD->Dealer execution actually happen;
- **dealer consultant** - diagnoses dealer condition/hot issue and helps improve capability;
- **leader/coach** - develops people instead of only issuing instruction;
- **innovator** - adapts activity/design/digital use to changing customer and market behavior;
- **controller** - monitors execution/result, evaluates and corrects.

## End-to-end loop

`Sense -> Diagnose -> Target -> Design strategy -> Mobilize -> Execute -> Monitor -> Evaluate -> Correct / Scale -> Develop people`.

### Sense
Read:
- market potential / economics / customer;
- local positive and negative issue;
- competitor movement;
- historical performance;
- dealer/people/network condition.

### Diagnose
Do not treat a performance gap as a cause. Decompose by area, product, dealer, activity, people and customer/process evidence until the problem point is isolated.

### Target
A target must be owned at the right level:
`province/MD -> kabupaten -> dealer -> people -> type/series/activity`.

### Design strategy
Specify:
- customer/segment;
- area/point;
- product;
- offer/program;
- activity;
- channel/network;
- people/PIC;
- digital/process enabler;
- monitoring metric.

### Mobilize
Use influence/presentation/coaching/change tools according to stakeholder barrier.

### Execute & control
Daily visibility where speed matters; monthly structured review for market, target, activity and capability.

### Improve
PICA/PDCA/DMAIC/Design Thinking are tools, not badges. Choose based on the problem.

## Two roles that must coexist

### Connector
“Ensure Dealer executes guidance and strategy from MD to achieve target.”

### Consultant
Ask:
- What is the dealer condition?
- What is the hot issue?
- What can improve?
- How can MD/SPV support dealer to achieve more?

Connector without consultant becomes compliance policing. Consultant without connector becomes advice without execution.

## Current weekly field-sensing layer — Market Info Tools (11 Aug 2026)

A current Sinsen rollout adds a weekly field-intelligence process at dealer × kecamatan grain. The operating purpose is to collect local market conditions, turn them into market insight, compare them with sales performance, and support early warning / action.

Current transition flow:
`Dealer -> MD Google Form -> Supervisor Sales Area validation -> AHM Market Info Tools portal`

This double-entry is a temporary quality gate, not the intended permanent architecture. The source says the future target is direct portal submission once dealer quality/consistency matures.

### Weekly sensing rules
- submit every week, latest Friday of the running week;
- submit even when there is no special event;
- multiple events can be recorded;
- capture dealer, kecamatan, event category/categories, event dates, and detailed narrative;
- same kecamatan/event can be reported by multiple dealers because the analytical key remains dealer-specific;
- use the event as contextual/hypothesis evidence, not automatic causal proof of sales performance.
