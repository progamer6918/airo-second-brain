## Source-derived module — Network Territory Sustainability Operating System
_Reconstruction module: `B03` / `NETWORK_TERRITORY_SUSTAINABILITY_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`_

This synthesis compiles the six Batch03 decks into one reasoning model. It does **not** make 2023 thresholds current.

## The system

**KPI Highlight** provides the top-level commercial outcome map: RS, MS, winning area, people/network, CRM/CSL, activities, digitalization, dealer groups and financing sustainability.

**Channel Management** answers: *do we have the right physical network coverage and are new/replacement/upgrade channels being delivered on time and economically?*

**Territory Management (TTM)** answers: *given the network, which area is each outlet responsible for, what should each territory contribute, and are activities/salespeople deployed to produce it?*

**NOS** answers: *does the network deliver a consistent customer experience through premises, people and process?*

**My Hero** is a people-enablement mechanism: distribute knowledge/tools, test knowledge, expose utilization/performance to Kacab, and focus coaching.

**INS** wraps the network into sustainability: quantitative performance/potential + qualitative owner/business/financial/regeneration risk + NOS + customer satisfaction + digital capability.

## A practical diagnostic sequence

When an area/dealer underperforms, do not jump directly to promotion.

1. **Outcome:** Which RS/MS/winning-area metric is weak? Is the issue total, segment, type or territory?
2. **Coverage:** Is the channel footprint adequate? Are new/replacement/upgrade/POS plans late or mismatched to market need?
3. **Territory:** Is target ownership by kecamatan/ring clear? Is the dealer winning Location/Ring1? Is cross-coverage masking weakness?
4. **Activity & people:** Is enough activity happening in the right territory, and is salesperson productivity adequate?
5. **Service:** Does NOS reveal premises/process/people breakdown that can reduce conversion, satisfaction or loyalty?
6. **Enablement:** Are salespeople actually using knowledge/recommendation tools, and do quiz/qualification results show capability gaps?
7. **Sustainability:** Is the dealer commercially weak because of deeper owner, succession, financial, customer or digital capability issues?
8. **Corrective loop:** turn evidence into PICA, monitor recovery, and recheck the relevant KPI family.

## Important tensions

- **More channels can reduce sustainability** if volume/economics are insufficient.
- **More activity can be wasteful** if placed outside priority territory or if salesperson productivity is weak.
- **High sales can coexist with fragile sustainability** when owner succession, cash flow, customer experience or process capability is weak.
- **High app login is not the goal**; capability and execution are.
- **NOS score is not the end state**; the objective is consistent service and continuous improvement.

## Currentness resolver

- NOS rules → resolve against `NOS/2026/*` current-standard workbooks.
- actual territory/ring mapping → resolve against latest dated business-memory source; the known ring mapping may be historical.
- current My Hero features/utilization → require a newer source.
- current channel/POS standards → require newer Netdev/POS authority.

## Source-derived module — Nos 2026 Network Operating System
_Reconstruction module: `B04` / `NOS_2026_NETWORK_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`_

## Core mental model
NOS 2026 is best read as a **network operating system**, not a building-cleanliness checklist. It asks whether a network can repeatedly deliver the intended customer experience through three auditable dimensions:

1. **Premises** — the physical/digital environment and tools exist in the required condition.
2. **Process** — roles execute the intended customer and operating journey.
3. **People** — staffing, training, tools, appearance, competency and role ratios are sufficient.

Those dimensions are applied separately to **H1 sales** and **H23 aftersales**, then adapted by network layer: BigWing, Wing, Regular H123, Regular H1, AHASS H23, POS, and BTL.

## Why this matters for diagnosis
A bad NOS result is not automatically a facilities problem. The same customer symptom can originate in:
- Premises: missing/poor facility, media, tool, display, system access.
- Process: step skipped, wrong sequence, no follow-up, no consent, no monitoring.
- People: understaffing, wrong role-sharing, training gap, tool/login gap, weak competency.

Therefore a NOS gap should be converted into **specific evidence → root-cause hypothesis → PICA → recheck**, not a generic beautification action.

## H1 system
The H1 standard spans discovery → dealing → purchase → credit/indent → payment → delivery → first-call/CRM follow-up, supported by warehouse, PDI, NMS, Customer Apps, MyHero, privacy consent, and digital/physical sales tools.

## H23 system
The H23 standard spans booking/reminder → arrival/SA → repair → extra-work approval → final inspection → payment → follow-up, plus parts/hotline order, tools, warehouse, people ratios, PPE and EV handling.

## Current 2026 strategy signals
The supplied current standard explicitly integrates **Reshape Network, digitalization, NMS/NMS Mobile, Customer Apps, MyHero, loyalty, EV, privacy/data consent, CRM and people/training**. These should be treated as current supplied NOS logic, unlike 2023 numeric examples.

## Source-derived module — Nos 2026 Scoring And Gating Model
_Reconstruction module: `B04` / `NOS_2026_SCORING_AND_GATING_MODEL.md`_

## Answer states
Final network checklists use: **Exist Good / Exist Not Good / Not Exist / N/A**. `N/A` participates in completion but is excluded from scored denominator.

## Sheet scoring patterns reconstructed from formulas
- **Premises:** Good = +1, Exist Not Good = -1, Not Exist = 0.
- **People:** Good = 2, Exist Not Good = 1, Not Exist = 0.
- **Process:** Good = 1; non-good states are zero/negative depending on sheet implementation. H1 Process in Regular H123 explicitly scores Good=1 and Exist Not Good=0; H23 Process follows the premises-like penalty pattern in the inspected formula set.

The point is not to mix raw percentages between dimensions without respecting their sheet-specific weighting.

## Audit grade thresholds
The Result Summary table encodes:
- Bronze: 0%–59.9%
- Silver: 60%–69.9%
- Gold: 70%–89.9%
- Platinum: 90%–100%

## Completion
A dimension is `Complete` only when every indicator row has one answer state selected (Good / Not Good / Not Exist / N/A). Completeness is separate from quality grade.

## Mandatory grade ceiling
Checklist rows can be tagged `P`, `G P`, or `S G P`. When a mandatory-tagged row is in a failing state (Exist Not Good or Not Exist), helper formulas convert the tag to a grade ceiling:
- violation on `P` item → mandatory result at best **Gold**;
- violation on `G P` item → at best **Silver**;
- violation on `S G P` item → **Bronze**.
- no mandatory violation → **Platinum** mandatory result.

This is a **gate**, not merely an extra point deduction.

## Final grade
Each dimension combines audit grade and mandatory grade through an explicit matrix that effectively chooses the worse grade. Overall Result Summary then combines the dimension-level audit percentages and mandatory grades and again applies the lower-grade logic.

## Diagnostic implication
A high raw percentage can still produce a low final grade if a critical mandatory item fails. Therefore review must always separate:
1. completion;
2. audit percentage;
3. mandatory violations;
4. final grade.

## Formula caveat
Some workbook formulas are stored/parsed with indexed-sheet references (`[10]Result Summary`, `[11]H23 People`). This batch preserves the intended logic but does not assert that every blank template will recalculate cleanly in every engine.

## Source-derived module — Nos 2026 Network Layer Differentiation
_Reconstruction module: `B04` / `NOS_2026_NETWORK_LAYER_DIFFERENTIATION.md`_

## Do not use one checklist for every outlet
The supplied current standard explicitly separates network types. The first routing question for any NOS review is therefore: **what is the canonical network layer?**

| Layer | Scope | Distinguishing logic |
|---|---|---|
| BigWing | H1 + H23 | Full premium / Big Bike layer: BBM/BBC/BBA, premium lounge, specialized Big Bike pits/tools, heavy repair, dyno, premium journey |
| Wing | H1 + H23 | Wing Corner, Wing Sales People, premium presentation/customer handling, selected premium H23 layer |
| Regular H123 | H1 + H23 | Full regular sales + aftersales baseline |
| Regular H1 | H1 only | Sales network standard without H23 dimensions |
| AHASS H23 | H23 only | Aftersales-only standard |
| POS H1/H2 | POS context | Compact satellite sales/service operating standard |
| BTL H1/H23 | temporary activity | Event/exhibition/roadshow operating standard |

## Decision rule
Never interpret `N/A`, missing dimensions, or headcount requirements before resolving the network layer. A requirement that is mandatory for BigWing may not exist for Regular; H23-only outlets should not be penalized for missing H1 dimensions.

## Source-derived module — Nos 2026 H1 H23 Customer Journey
_Reconstruction module: `B04` / `NOS_2026_H1_H23_CUSTOMER_JOURNEY.md`_

## H1 journey
1. Arrival / greeting / need identification.
2. Prospect/appointment context and prior relationship.
3. Product exploration with MyHero/Customer Apps/NMS support.
4. After-sales and warranty explanation.
5. Accessories/apparel and riding test where applicable.
6. Negotiation: availability, current sales program, cash/credit parity, discount approval.
7. SPK / personal-data consent / credit / indent.
8. Payment and document status.
9. Delivery: PDI, unit explanation, warranty/KPB, safety.
10. First call / CRM / service reminder / next prospecting cycle.

## H23 journey
1. Booking / service reminders.
2. Arrival / queue / loyalty recognition.
3. SA diagnosis and repair authorization.
4. Workshop execution and extra-work approval.
5. Final inspection.
6. Final explanation before payment.
7. Payment / handover.
8. Satisfaction follow-up / next booking reminder.
9. Parts and hotline-order flows when stock is unavailable.

## Cross-H1/H23 loop
The standard explicitly contains H1→H2 and H2→H1 follow-up / cross-selling data flows, service reminders from sales data, and CRM distribution. This creates a closed customer lifecycle rather than independent sales and service silos.

## Source-derived module — Nos 2026 Reshape Digital Ev Loyalty
_Reconstruction module: `B04` / `NOS_2026_RESHAPE_DIGITAL_EV_LOYALTY.md`_

## Reshape Network
The change-control workbook explicitly requires every changed checklist item to align with **RESHAPE NETWORK**. Concrete implications in the final standard include dealer-group warehouse centralization, dealer-specific signage/stock separation inside centralized storage, group-level delivery/PDI capacity logic, and continued ownership of stock by outlet even when physical operations are shared.

## Digitalization
Digital is embedded in the operating process, not treated as a side channel:
- NMS / NMS Mobile for prospecting, SPK, stock, service, booking, indent, discount approval and follow-up.
- Customer Apps for booking, tracking, loyalty, product/service information and customer engagement.
- MyHero as front-line product/sales tool.
- Digital PDI, digital appreciation card, digital signage/payment, Google Business Profile.

## Data privacy
Customer-data consent appears inside H1 purchase/SPK and broader all-role data-protection processes. A compliant digital journey is therefore not simply "data captured"; consent and permitted usage are part of the operating standard.

## Loyalty
Customer Apps loyalty tiers influence physical/service handling such as Champion/Racer cover-jok and premium parking/facilities. Loyalty therefore bridges digital identity and physical network experience.

## EV
EV readiness is distributed across signage, corner/display, Honda Care, KSU storage, APD, class-D fire protection, charger/battery handling, low-voltage mechanic PPE, service/indent flows and product education. Treat EV as a cross-domain readiness layer, not a single premises item.

## Source-derived module — Netdev 2026 Operating System
_Reconstruction module: `B05` / `NETDEV_2026_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`_

## Core model

Network Development 2026 is a system for making the network adaptive, relevant, efficient, and sustainable. It integrates:

- customer satisfaction/experience via NOS;
- coverage/productivity via ND Plan and TTM;
- early warning via INS;
- future business transformation via Reshape;
- monthly execution discipline via BEST.

## Master loop

```text
Market drivers / customer data
        ↓
Area potential and concern identification
        ↓
Coverage design and network mapping
        ↓
Integrated TTM activity and people assignment
        ↓
Network performance and sustainability review
        ↓
Reshape / transformation / sharing function if needed
        ↓
BEST monthly execution control
        ↓
PICA / TTFU / next strategy
```

## Why this matters

Before Batch05, WorkDesk knew NOS/INS/TTM in pieces. Batch05 adds the higher-order system: these are not isolated programs. They are linked by strategy.

- NOS defines minimum operating standard and customer journey standard.
- ND Plan designs coverage and productivity action.
- TTM converts coverage into territory-level activity.
- INS monitors sustainability and risk.
- Reshape changes cost/productivity/process model.
- BEST checks monthly dealer execution.

## Decision logic

When an area or dealer underperforms, do not jump directly to `add activity`. Diagnose which layer failed:

1. Market/readiness: is the target area actually potential by PDRB/CDB/market contribution/growth?
2. Coverage: is Honda presence enough by dealer/POS/AHASS/ring?
3. Activity: are H1/H2 activity and FLP/mechanic assignment integrated and scheduled?
4. Productivity: do SM/SC/mechanics hit minimum productivity/class expectations?
5. Standard: do NOS/BTL/POS requirements support customer journey?
6. Digital/CRM: are NMS, register user, leads, CRM, CDB, and DCSL used correctly?
7. Sustainability: does INS/Network Classification show risk?
8. Reshape: should process/premises/people transformation or sharing function be applied?
9. Control: what did BEST monthly data reveal, and what PICA/TTFU must happen?

## Source-derived module — Reshape Network 2026 Model
_Reconstruction module: `B05` / `RESHAPE_NETWORK_2026_MODEL.md`_

## Purpose

Reshape Network changes how the network operates so that cost efficiency, productivity, customer experience, and digital readiness improve together.

## Roadmap logic

The 2026 deck presents a roadmap from 2023 to 2029/2030 with rising productivity and cost-efficiency targets. The exact percentages are roadmap examples/targets in the template; they should be validated against current AHM/MD target authority before use as current KPI.

## Core components

1. **MD Transformation**
   - Main Dealer internal transformation initiatives.
   - Benefit and timeline tracking.

2. **Network Transformation**
   - Process / Premises / People change.
   - Before-after method and calculation.
   - Quantitative efficiency and qualitative benefit.
   - Implementation status and evidence.

3. **Sharing Function**
   - CRM people sharing/centralization/network transformation.
   - Admin penagihan, admin STNK/BPKB, warehouse, PDI/delivery support where applicable.
   - Requires process clarity, workload monitoring, efficiency/productivity proof, and change management.

4. **Shared Logistics / Centralized Warehouse**
   - Evaluates dealer-group warehouse/logistics with before-after cost, lead time, manpower productivity, investment, distance, and flow process.

5. **Incubate Reshape Network**
   - Uses dealer reference and dealer incubate.
   - Observes people, process, technology, policy, H1/H23/CRM flows, and system integration.
   - Translates observation into development plan.

## Good reshape decision

A reshape initiative is not validated by implementation status alone. It must show:

- before state;
- after state;
- calculation method;
- qualitative benefit;
- efficiency/productivity impact;
- implementation percentage;
- evidence;
- PICA for gap;
- next strategy/timeline.

## Source-derived module — Best Monthly Operating Control System
_Reconstruction module: `B05` / `BEST_MONTHLY_OPERATING_CONTROL_SYSTEM.md`_

## Function

BEST is the recurring control rhythm that makes dealer execution visible. It takes plans from Sales, Logistics, HC3/TSD/People/Marcomm and turns them into dealer-level TTFU.

## Typical monthly layers

- Sales condition and monthly calendar/theme.
- BTL/showroom/roadshow/twin-date/event directions.
- EV focus and activation approach.
- Sales support/MSW socialization and evidence expectations.
- Register motor vs SSU, register user, lead conversion, CRM/NMS handling.
- Logistics: indent accuracy, truck/stock/run-out, PDI digital.
- H2/H3/TSD controls such as KPB, LCR, unit entry, parts/oil, dashboard usage.
- HC3 controls: CDB quality, mystery calling/booking, CSL/GBP, customer movement.
- People development: productivity, turnover, MyHero, SEIN, SIPEDE, content quality.

## How WorkDesk should use BEST

When a case mentions recent dealer execution, do not only consult training frameworks. Check the applicable monthly operating-review context:

- What was the current event/theme?
- Which execution accuracy metric was being monitored?
- Was there a repeated TTFU from previous month?
- Which system was the source of truth: SEEDS, NMS, webconsole, PDI digital, CRM, Base/worklog?
- Is the issue in activity, data accuracy, follow-up, conversion, productivity, or compliance?

## Currentness rule

BEST decks are month-specific. July 2026 BEST is high authority for July/August operational context in this cluster. Older BEST decks are historical trend and operating rhythm unless a repeated rule is also present in the latest applicable source.

## Source-derived module — Best Jan2026 Operating Control
_Reconstruction module: `B09` / `BEST_JAN2026_OPERATING_CONTROL.md`_

January 2026 BEST reinforces a monthly-control model in which Sales, Marketing/Promotion, Logistics and HC3/CRM are read together.

A dealer may appear weak in retail but the control questions should branch across:
- target and market condition;
- activity/BTL execution;
- register motor/user and digital adoption;
- program socialization;
- content/community selling;
- indent/logistics accuracy;
- CDB and CRM funnel quality;
- Contact Center / Mystery Calling / Mystery Booking.

The key insight is **cross-functional diagnosis**: a sales symptom may originate in marketing execution, lead hygiene, logistics, network process or customer handling. Monthly BEST is a control surface for locating the weak link.

Currentness is mandatory: January 2026 values are a historical snapshot once later monthly sources exist.
