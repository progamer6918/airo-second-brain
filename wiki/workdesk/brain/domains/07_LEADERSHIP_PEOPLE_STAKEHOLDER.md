## Source-derived module — Advanced Area Supervisor Operating System
_Reconstruction module: `B08` / `ADVANCED_AREA_SUPERVISOR_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`_

## Core identity

An advanced Area SPV is simultaneously:

- **business sensor** - understands market, dealer, customer, competitor and performance evidence;
- **strategy translator** - turns MD intent into area/dealer-specific target and action;
- **connector** - makes cross-function and MD->Dealer execution actually happen;
- **dealer consultant** - diagnoses dealer condition/hot issue and helps improve capability;
- **leader/coach** - develops people instead of only issuing instruction;
- **innovator** - adapts activity/design/digital use to changing customer and market behavior;
- **controller** - monitors execution/result, evaluates and corrects.

## End-to-end loop

`Sense -> Diagnose -> Target -> Design strategy -> Mobilize -> Execute -> Monitor -> Evaluate -> Correct / Scale -> Develop people`.

### Sense
Read:
- market potential / economics / customer;
- local positive and negative issue;
- competitor movement;
- historical performance;
- dealer/people/network condition.

### Diagnose
Do not treat a performance gap as a cause. Decompose by area, product, dealer, activity, people and customer/process evidence until the problem point is isolated.

### Target
A target must be owned at the right level:
`province/MD -> kabupaten -> dealer -> people -> type/series/activity`.

### Design strategy
Specify:
- customer/segment;
- area/point;
- product;
- offer/program;
- activity;
- channel/network;
- people/PIC;
- digital/process enabler;
- monitoring metric.

### Mobilize
Use influence/presentation/coaching/change tools according to stakeholder barrier.

### Execute & control
Daily visibility where speed matters; monthly structured review for market, target, activity and capability.

### Improve
PICA/PDCA/DMAIC/Design Thinking are tools, not badges. Choose based on the problem.

## Two roles that must coexist

### Connector
“Ensure Dealer executes guidance and strategy from MD to achieve target.”

### Consultant
Ask:
- What is the dealer condition?
- What is the hot issue?
- What can improve?
- How can MD/SPV support dealer to achieve more?

Connector without consultant becomes compliance policing. Consultant without connector becomes advice without execution.

## Source-derived module — Coaching And People Development
_Reconstruction module: `B08` / `COACHING_AND_PEOPLE_DEVELOPMENT.md`_

## Why coaching belongs in WorkDesk

A supervisor cannot solve every dealer problem by personally supplying the answer. Coaching converts diagnosis into capability building.

## Core competencies

1. Being Present.
2. Active Listening.
3. Questioning.

## GROW

- Goal - desired result / priority.
- Reality - current situation, evidence, obstacles, attempts.
- Options - alternatives/resources/support.
- Will - exact commitment and timing.

## Session operating flow

### Opening
- prepare with prior data/context;
- build relationship;
- clarify confidentiality;
- define duration;
- set agenda and desired session outcome.

### Digging
- use open questions;
- listen for key words;
- move from story to reality/gap/options;
- stimulate, do not hijack the thinking.

### Closing
- move from exploration to action;
- record 1-3 actions;
- confirm commitment;
- define follow-up/next session.

## Coaching vs directing

Use direct instruction when:
- safety/compliance is non-negotiable;
- the person lacks minimum information;
- urgent correction cannot wait.

Use coaching when:
- the person has enough context to think;
- ownership/capability matters;
- the problem requires reflection and choice.

Advanced supervision needs both.

## Source-derived module — Communication Influence Presentation
_Reconstruction module: `B08` / `COMMUNICATION_INFLUENCE_PRESENTATION.md`_

## Influence is barrier-specific

Five source tools:
- Social Proof;
- Liking;
- Contrast;
- Time Machine;
- Higher Purpose.

Use them to reduce a specific stakeholder barrier, not to decorate a presentation.

## Data -> Insight

Meeting presentation should convert raw analysis into a small number of decisions/insights.

Source principles:
- insightful;
- confidence-building;
- simple.

## Nine-slide narrative

### Empathy
1. Status Quo.
2. Observation.
3. Story.

### Curiosity
4. Insight.
5. Opportunity.
6. Analogy.

### Confidence
7. Solution.
8. Advantages.
9. Ethos.

## Supervisory presentation rule

A good meeting deck should make the audience able to answer:
- What is happening?
- Why does it matter?
- What did we learn that was not obvious?
- What opportunity/action follows?
- Why should we believe this action?
- Who owns the next step?

One message per slide is a simplification rule, not permission to omit decision-critical evidence.

## Source-derived module — Change And Organizational Transformation
_Reconstruction module: `B08` / `CHANGE_AND_ORGANIZATIONAL_TRANSFORMATION.md`_

## Strategy-to-performance prerequisites

The Tanadi source uses five elements:
`Vision + Skills + Incentives + Resources + Action Plan`.

Failure modes when one is missing:
- no Vision -> Confusion;
- no Skills -> Anxiety;
- no Incentives -> Gradual Change;
- no Resources -> Frustration;
- no Action Plan -> Not Implemented.

## Four hurdles

- Cognitive / understanding.
- Resource.
- Motivational.
- Political.

Before blaming “resistance,” identify which hurdle is actually blocking adoption.

## Change sequence principles

- create urgency;
- communicate trust and capability;
- use stories and visible examples;
- alter the environment/process, not only tell people to behave differently;
- create short-term wins;
- shrink change into manageable steps;
- embed from forced behavior -> capable -> habitual -> culture.

## Culture diagnosis

Compare **espoused values** with **observed behavior**. If they diverge, the culture problem is operational, not a poster problem.

Humanocracy principles add a design direction: ownership, meritocracy, markets, community, openness, experimentation and balance.

## Experiment rule

When possible, test organizational changes with a hypothesis, treatment/control comparison, defined measure and bounded trial. This reduces debate by opinion.

## Source-derived module — Critical Creative Innovation
_Reconstruction module: `B08` / `CRITICAL_CREATIVE_INNOVATION.md`_

## Ambidextrous thinking

The source pairs:
- **critical** -> exploitation / improvement;
- **creative** -> exploration / new possibilities.

Innovation needs both.

Creative without critical screening produces exciting but fragile ideas. Critical without creative exploration optimizes the old system forever.

## Improvement side

Continuous improvement uses PDCA and standardization:
`Plan -> Do -> Check -> Act -> standardize -> improve again`.

## Creative side

Questions:
- WHAT IF?
- “Bagaimana kalau?”

SCAMPER:
- Substitute;
- Combine;
- Adopt/adapt;
- Modify/Magnify;
- Put to other use;
- Eliminate;
- Rearrange/Reverse.

Creative association should borrow mechanisms from other contexts/industries, then adapt rather than copy blindly.

## Critical side

Ask:
- Is the pattern consistent?
- What is the evidence?
- What are the implications?
- Is it rational/realistic?
- What are the facts?
- Why?

Idea selection must explicitly consider applicability and risk.

## Innovation loop

`Problem/opportunity -> expand possibilities -> screen evidence/risk -> prototype/test -> measure -> standardize or discard`.

## Source-derived module — Execution Discipline Time Focus
_Reconstruction module: `B08` / `EXECUTION_DISCIPLINE_TIME_FOCUS.md`_

## Core shift

Time management = **self-management**.

`Manage Self -> Manage Activity -> Manage Time`.

## Activity decisions

Separate importance from urgency. For a supervisor, important-not-urgent work often includes:
- market/dealer planning;
- coaching;
- relationship building;
- capability development;
- prevention;
- preparing upcoming activity/decision.

If urgent work dominates continuously, the system becomes crisis management.

## Weekly planning

`Roles & annual goals -> Big Rocks -> weekly schedule`.

This makes project/activity time explicit instead of hoping it appears.

## Focus management

Training recommendations include:
- one task per moment;
- silent/distance phone where possible;
- batch notifications/email;
- 30-45 minute deep-work interval;
- deliberate breaks.

## Integrity

The system only works if commitments on the weekly plan are treated as real commitments. Do not overfill the calendar and then call deviations “unavoidable.”

## Source anomaly guard

The “effective manager” quadrant percentages shown (28/33/24/18) add to 103%. They are not used as a target allocation formula.

## Source-derived module — Customer Centric Leadership And Moments
_Reconstruction module: `B08` / `CUSTOMER_CENTRIC_LEADERSHIP_AND_MOMENTS.md`_

## Customer centricity is tension management

Three tensions from the source:
- stability vs change;
- openness vs privacy;
- close vs professional.

A strong customer system resolves these tensions deliberately rather than choosing one extreme.

## Trust foundations

`Empathy + Effective Communication + Psychological Safety`.

Operational translation:
- understand need/emotion before designing treatment;
- communicate clearly and verify understanding;
- create an environment where customer/team can surface truth without fear.

## Satisfaction is not the end state

NPS distinguishes Promoter, Passive and Detractor. The source also emphasizes emotional + rational/business needs and CASH expectations:
`Convenience, Action, Speed, Hassle Free`.

## Power moments

Design customer/dealer/people experience around critical moments:
- **Elevation** - make ordinary interaction noticeably better.
- **Insight** - help someone realize something important quickly.
- **Pride** - recognize achievement/identity.
- **Connection** - create shared meaning/relationship.

Potential dealer moments:
- first inquiry;
- test ride;
- credit approval/problem;
- unit delivery;
- first service;
- complaint recovery;
- loyalty milestone;
- staff/dealer achievement.

## Decision rule

Do not optimize every touchpoint equally. Find moments with disproportionate effect on confidence, loyalty, action or advocacy, then design the mechanism and evidence.

## Source-derived module — Basic Supervisor Operating System
_Reconstruction module: `B09` / `BASIC_SUPERVISOR_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`_

Batch09 closes the Basic soft-skill, project-transfer and best-practice layer around the hard-skill brain reconstructed in earlier batches.

## The combined operating system
A supervisor is expected to manage five loops simultaneously:

1. **Think and prioritize**  
   Roles/goals -> Important vs Urgent -> Big Rocks -> weekly execution.

2. **Diagnose and improve**  
   Current vs Desired gap -> hypotheses -> evidence -> root cause -> solution -> PIC/deadline -> control.

3. **Work through people**  
   Listening -> empathy -> clear/assertive communication -> conflict resolution -> appreciation -> coaching/relationship.

4. **Translate strategy into dealer routines**  
   target/forecast -> territory/activity -> sales/stock -> Niguri -> dealer review -> NOS -> PICA.

5. **Communicate for action**  
   audience/purpose -> clear story -> evidence -> concrete ask -> follow-up.

The project-governance sources add a sixth requirement: **implementation must be evidenced and sustainable**, not merely presented.

## Supervisor mental model
`Professional competence = analytical discipline + people execution + communication + sustained control.`

Hard-skill analysis without people/communication does not execute. People leadership without data/root-cause discipline becomes opinion. Presentation without implementation/control becomes theatre.

## Source-derived module — Change Resilience And Persuasion
_Reconstruction module: `B09` / `CHANGE_RESILIENCE_AND_PERSUASION.md`_

Change work operates at two levels.

### System level
Create speed across People, Process and Technology; remove complacency; collaborate; then use DMAIC or another evidence-based improvement loop to define, measure, analyze, improve and control.

### Human level
People may need help reframing rejection, maintaining perseverance and replacing internal narratives that keep them stuck. Persuasion should start from the other person's world: listen, ask, acknowledge and let the issue be fully expressed before advocating a solution.

## Guard
Resilience does not replace diagnosis. Motivation without capability/resources/process is not a change plan. Combine this source with the stronger Change model reconstructed in Batch08 (`Vision + Skills + Incentives + Resources + Action Plan`).

## Source-derived module — Interpersonal Stakeholder And Meeting System
_Reconstruction module: `B09` / `INTERPERSONAL_STAKEHOLDER_AND_MEETING_SYSTEM.md`_

## Before talking
1. Identify the stakeholder's pressure, goals and expectations.
2. Separate observed data from the meaning/assumptions you have attached to it.
3. Listen for the full issue before presenting your solution.

## During interaction
- Listen actively; manage self-focus and prior assumptions.
- Use empathy without surrendering the business requirement.
- Communicate clearly across verbal, vocal and non-verbal channels.
- Use assertiveness: direct and respectful, not passive or aggressive.
- In conflict, find common ground and build a fair solution rather than trying to win the argument.

## For meetings
A meeting earns its cost only when it has a clear objective, relevant participants, prepared agenda, bounded time, collaborative discussion and documented follow-up.

## For dealer/fincoy/manager relationships
Switch deliberately among tactical, strategic and relational communication. Maintain information flow and reliability, but remain solution-oriented. The Area-SPV case adds the principle of complementary strengths + common mission + reciprocity.

## Source-derived module — Presentation And Visual Pitch System
_Reconstruction module: `B09` / `PRESENTATION_AND_VISUAL_PITCH_SYSTEM.md`_

## Human presentation layer
Start from **purpose and audience**. The message should answer what the audience needs to understand/decide/do.

Control nine practical dimensions: readiness, purpose, audience, tone/voice, concreteness, conduct/body language, interest, storytelling and enthusiasm.

## Visual/story design layer
The TS compilation adds a useful design question: what kind of meeting is this?
- information delivery;
- decision making;
- problem solving;
- new idea;
- sales pitch.

A strong pitch can then move through:
`Title -> Common Ground -> Coming Problem -> Emotional Win -> False Hope -> Audacious Reality -> Why We Can -> Call to Action -> Early Benefits -> Long Win.`

## WorkDesk rule
Do not decorate a weak argument. First make the analysis defensible; then use story/visual design to make the reasoning easier to understand, remember and act on.
