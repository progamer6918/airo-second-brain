# Canonical Professional Brain — Reconciliation v0.1

The 80-source baseline resolves into an integrated operating brain, not 80 independent summaries.

## 1. Sense the business

**Market / economy / territory / customer / competitor / dealer facts** → determine what actually changed and where.

Core concepts:
- market sensing and economic drivers;
- market share and denominator logic;
- area/kabupaten/kecamatan/ring contribution;
- customer profile / jobs / needs / journey;
- competitor and pricing/financing conditions;
- dealer/network capacity and sustainability.

## 2. Localize the problem or opportunity

Use decompositions such as:
- Main Dealer → Kabupaten → Dealer → Series;
- Main Dealer → Segment/Type → Kabupaten → Dealer;
- area/territory → ring → dealer → activity → salesperson;
- customer funnel → lead → contacted → deal → retail;
- network type → H1/H23 → Premises/Process/People.

Do not diagnose before localization.

## 3. Explain the mechanism

Test causes by **change, contrast and magnitude**. A valid cause must explain why the outcome changed, why comparable areas/products behaved differently, and whether its magnitude can explain the gap.

Mechanism families:
- external/market;
- competitor;
- Honda execution;
- supply/stock/distribution;
- pricing/financing;
- territory/coverage/activity;
- people/productivity;
- customer experience/CRM;
- network standard/operations;
- leadership/change/execution discipline.

## 4. Translate opportunity into target and supply

Potential market → M/S objective → target Polreg/RS → dealer target → salesperson/activity requirement → forecast feasibility → Niguri → distribution/stock.

Forecast is not a passive guess. Source logic: **Pattern + Unexplained**, with seasonality, trend/growth, new model, competition, reasonable M/S, economy, weather, buying power, disaster, activity/promotion and stock.

## 5. Execute through the network

Channel Management + TTM + NOS + INS + NetDev/Reshape + BEST form one network operating system:

coverage design → territory ownership → people/activity → customer journey standard → sustainability risk → network transformation → monthly operating control.

## 6. Activate and convert customers

Physical and digital activation share one conversion chain:

territory need → activity/VE → visitor/traffic → unique lead → contacted → prospect → deal → SPK/SO → retail contribution → evidence → evaluation.

CRM adds CDB quality, profiling, follow-up and loyalty/retention.

## 7. Solve problems and improve

Use the method that matches uncertainty:
- PDCA: operating control loop;
- PICA/PPS: known performance gap, isolate root cause and corrective action;
- DMAIC: systematic project improvement where measurement/control matter;
- Design Thinking: user/problem/solution uncertainty;
- business case: connect market insight, target, resource, activity, budget and expected benefit.

## 8. Mobilize people and stakeholders

Supervisor is both **connector** and **dealer consultant**.

Capabilities:
- listening/empathy/assertiveness;
- coaching via GROW;
- negotiation via BATNA / reservation / aspiration / ZOPA;
- influence and presentation;
- critical + creative thinking;
- change diagnosis (Vision, Skills, Incentives, Resources, Action Plan);
- meeting discipline and follow-up;
- weekly focus / Big Rocks.

## 9. Close the loop

Dealer Review is a strategy diary, not merely visit preparation.

Monthly/periodic control should connect:
market → target → sales/stock → activity → leads/CRM → network/NOS → people → finance → PICA → recheck → next strategy.

## 10. Currentness is a first-class reasoning dimension

Never retrieve “latest-looking” knowledge blindly. Resolve:
requested date → applicable source → revisions/supersession → authority → conflict state.

A historical example remains valuable for reasoning but cannot silently become a current operating rule.
