## Domain availability

Typical dealer files separate H1 and/or H23 into Premises, Process and People plus Result Summary. Applicability differs by network type.

## Operating rule

Never copy a requirement because it exists in another network workbook. Resolve:

1. network type;
2. H1/H23 scope;
3. Premises/Process/People domain;
4. layer/mandatory/mark;
5. exact indicator;
6. evidence and N/A eligibility.
