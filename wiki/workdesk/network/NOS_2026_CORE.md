# NOS 2026 — Core Model

## 🍼 10 detik

## 2026 direction

Guidance explicitly requires checklist changes (update/delete/merge/new) to align with **RESHAPE NETWORK**, and flags loyalty, EV charger needs, Reshape items, internal people/training concerns and digitalization.

## Analysis flow

`network type → applicable checklist/domain → exact item → mandatory? → actual condition → gap → PI → CA/PICA → verify`

Do not apply one checklist to every outlet.
