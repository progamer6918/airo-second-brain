# WorkDesk Knowledge Map v1

WorkDesk v1 memakai tiga memory planes.

## A. Professional Brain - "Gimana cara mikir?"

Target compiled domains:

1. Dealer Performance Intelligence
2. Demand, Forecast & Stock Planning
3. Market & Area Intelligence
4. Pricing & Financing Logic
5. Sales Funnel, Activity & People Management
6. NOS & Network Operations
7. Customer Intelligence & CRM
8. Leadership, Coaching & Organizational Change
9. Negotiation & Problem Solving
10. Analytics & Power BI Data Logic
11. Presentation & Business Case Engineering
12. Owner Applied Case Library

## B. Business Memory - "Apa yang memang terjadi?"

Private by default:

- dealer/product/territory masters;
- historical market and retail;
- stock snapshots/history;
- commercial program/MSW history;
- future case-specific funnel, activity, competitor, finance, and other operational datasets.

Public WorkDesk stores the **logic for using these data**, not raw sensitive values.

## C. Case / Delta Memory - "Apa yang baru kita pelajari?"

Future input is incremental:

`new data / case → classify → digest → reconcile → use → persist only durable meaning`

The baseline brain is not rebuilt from zero whenever a new month, program, or case arrives.
