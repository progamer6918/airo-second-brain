# Influence, Coaching & Change

## 🍼 10 detik

Gunakan influence untuk membangun buy-in, coaching untuk membantu orang menemukan commitment, dan change management untuk memastikan organisasi benar-benar bergerak.

## Influence

Advanced BWI material teaches devices such as Social Proof, Liking, Contrast, Time Machine and Higher Purpose. Treat these as taught persuasion tools, not universal laws.

## Coaching

The material uses **GROW**:

`Goal → Reality → Options → Will`

Useful when the job is not simply giving an instruction, but making dealer/leader own the next action.

## Change diagnosis

External material includes a strategy-to-performance diagnostic:

`Vision + Skills + Incentives + Resources + Action Plan → Change`

Missing pieces can manifest as confusion, anxiety, gradual/weak change, frustration or non-implementation.

It also highlights cognitive, resource, motivational and political hurdles, plus small wins / shrink-the-change thinking.
