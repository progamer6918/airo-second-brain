# Supervisory Leadership & Organizational Execution

## 🍼 10 detik

ASS tidak cukup benar secara analitis. Ia harus membuat dealer/network **mengerti, mau, mampu dan benar-benar menjalankan** perubahan.

## Basic BWI capability

- active listening;
- effective communication;
- empathy;
- conflict resolution;
- assertiveness/respect;
- emotional intelligence;
- teamwork;
- presentation/storytelling;
- role/goal/time management;
- RCA;
- managing upward/relationship with boss.

## Advanced capability

Advanced external material adds influence/persuasion, coaching, creative thinking, customer/business thinking, organizational development and leading change.

## Business implication

When a good PICA is not executed, do not immediately conclude “dealer tidak mau”. Diagnose execution conditions such as clarity/vision, skill, incentive, resources, action plan, ownership and political/coordination hurdles.
