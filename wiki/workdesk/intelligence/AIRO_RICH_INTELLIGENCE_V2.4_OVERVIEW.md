# 🧠 AIRO WorkDesk Rich Intelligence v2.4 Overview

## Purpose
Externalize the Owner's professional brain into a structured decision-grade intelligence system, capturing case-driven deltas without replacing baseline operational memory.

## Intelligence Layers
1. **Baseline Layer**: Historical core business memory & operational digests.
2. **Case-Driven Delta Layer**: Provenance-preserving execution context (e.g. AHM Award Q3 2026).
3. **Synthesis & Reasoning Layer**: Multi-source reconciliation and decision rules.

## Confidence Model
- **Level 1 (High)**: Verified operational evidence & direct formal guidance.
- **Level 2 (Medium)**: Corroborated multi-source activity logs.
- **Level 3 (Low / Reference)**: Single-source reference materials & unverified claims.

## Source Authority Rule
Operational facts supersede reference materials. Provenance and authority level are recorded for every ingested unit.
