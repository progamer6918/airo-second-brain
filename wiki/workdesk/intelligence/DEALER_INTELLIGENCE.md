# 🏢 Dealer & Network Intelligence

## Entity Hierarchy
`Group` ➔ `Dealer` ➔ `POS (Point of Sales)`

## Relationships & Territory Mapping
- **Group Level**: Holding / Main Dealer ownership structure.
- **Dealer Level**: Main outlet with full H123 operational capability (BigWing / Wing / Regular H123).
- **POS Level**: Satellite point of sales outlets linked to parent dealer territory.
- **Territory Alignment**: Mapped against POLREG geographic filters and TTM network plans.
