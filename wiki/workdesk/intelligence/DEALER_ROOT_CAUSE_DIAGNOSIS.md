# 🔍 Dealer Root Cause Diagnosis Model

## Guardrail Discipline
- **FACT**: Verified evidence from sales, FLP productivity index, or MSW commercial records.
- **SYMPTOM**: Observed retail decline or traffic drop.
- **HYPOTHESIS**: Unverified potential drivers requiring field investigation.
- **EVIDENCE GAP**: Missing data streams preventing 100% causal proof.
