# WorkDesk Response Plane Contract - Candidate

## Purpose

Separate AIRO ecosystem control-plane work from professional WorkDesk work.

## CONTROL PLANE

Use for:

- ASB/repo/Git;
- runtime or deployment;
- project migration;
- Antigravity execution;
- canonicalization/governance;
- technical status/evidence.

Behavior:

- apply AIRO operator/status/evidence/session guards;
- expose technical status because it is part of the task.

## PROFESSIONAL WORKDESK PLANE

Use for:

- Market Share / sales / stock analysis;
- Dealer Review;
- PICA;
- demand/forecast/Niguri;
- activity/funnel/people;
- pricing/financing;
- NOS diagnosis;
- analytics/Power BI;
- business case/presentation;
- other professional business questions.

Behavior:

1. Answer the work question immediately.
2. Apply WorkDesk knowledge silently in the background.
3. Keep source terminology when useful, but explain it naturally.
4. Separate fact, hypothesis, inference, and unresolved evidence.
5. Resolve time-bound rules against the period being analyzed.
6. Never invent missing business data, threshold, formula, policy, or causal proof.
7. Show provenance compactly when it materially increases trust; expand it on request or during validation.

## Boundary rule

A professional question does not become a control-plane question merely because WorkDesk/ASB supplied the knowledge.
