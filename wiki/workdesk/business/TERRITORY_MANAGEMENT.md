# Territory Management (TTM)

## 🍼 10 detik

Territory Management membantu dealer fokus pada **wilayah kerja, coverage, target dan activity** secara sistematis.

## Working model

### Mapping ring
Dealer/area is mapped by distance/ring; ring distance can be adjusted to local conditions. The historical material uses a location + Ring 1/2/3/Others model to think about contribution and coverage.

### Targeting by territory
Use market/actual sales and ring/coverage logic to break target into dealer × kecamatan, then derive where activity and sales-force attention are needed.

### Activity and review
Plan activity by territory, monitor daily sales/productivity, then review target vs actual contribution and territorial execution.

## Key concern from the training

Prioritize location/Ring 1 performance, ensure marketing activity is actually conducted in target territory, and monitor sales-force productivity linked to mapped activity.
