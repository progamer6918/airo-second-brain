# Dealer Review

## 🍼 10 detik

Dealer Review adalah **management diary + diagnosis system**, bukan deck yang baru diisi saat ada kunjungan.

## Minimum thinking structure

### Market information
- positive/negative issues around the dealer;
- 1–3 months backward and forward;
- hyperlocal, not copy-paste from Main Dealer.

### Dealer profile / performance
- current-month forecast, not only last-month actual;
- people;
- cash/credit;
- sales/stock;
- segment/customer movement.

### Strategy
Translate market/dealer condition into forecast, sales/activity strategy, follow-up and development action.

## Advanced SPV review lens

Advanced material adds structured items such as target/achievement by type/people, stock days, cash-credit/fincoy status, hyperlocal market/potential, migration/RO indicators and deeper monitoring.

## Operating rule

Dealer Review should answer:

`what happened → why → where/which segment/dealer/people → what is controllable → what action → what evidence will prove improvement`
