# Sales Force Productivity

## 🍼 10 detik

Jangan hanya tanya “berapa sales people?”. Tanyakan **berapa orang aktif, produktivitasnya berapa, targetnya apa, activity-nya apa, dan coverage-nya cukup atau tidak**.

## Core operating questions

- current active salesman / sales counter count;
- target and achievement per person/type;
- productivity per person/team;
- target workload vs available people;
- need for additional people;
- activity per person and frequency;
- lead/funnel quality and follow-up;
- training/tools readiness;
- manager/leader monitoring discipline.

## Link to targeting

ASSDP target breakdown explicitly extends target from province/kabupaten/dealer down to sales force, with additional people/activity calculation where the gap requires it.

Advanced SPV material asks: contribution by sales force, number of people, productivity, whether additional SF is needed, activity/frequency, and target sales per activity.
