# Sales, Stock & Distribution Monitoring

## 🍼 10 detik

Jangan lihat sales tanpa stock, dan jangan lihat total MD tanpa breakdown area/dealer/type.

## Monitoring hierarchy

ASSDP Basic teaches three levels:

1. **Total Main Dealer** — stock MD + dealer + in-transit, all type and series.
2. **Kabupaten** — daily sales/stock M vs M-1; connect growth with economic drivers.
3. **Dealer** — drill down county movement into dealer and series contributors.

## Why

- detect where positive/negative growth originates;
- avoid stock composition imbalance;
- identify in-transit/availability issues;
- determine whether sales issue is market, dealer, series or stock-driven.

## Distribution mindset

Distribution should reflect sell-out capacity/potential, not only inventory requests. Demand-management/Niguri material reinforces balance between supply, sales plan and market opportunity.
