# Honda / Main Dealer Work Ecosystem

## 🍼 10 detik

Kerja ASS berada di tengah rantai **AHM → Main Dealer → Dealer/Network → Customer**, dengan dukungan sales, service, parts, customer care, financing dan systems.

## 🧑‍💼 Buat kerja

Jangan membaca performa area hanya sebagai retail sales. Dalam materi ASSDP/ND Plan, business review menyentuh beberapa domain sekaligus:

- **H1** — sales/unit business;
- **H2** — service/AHASS;
- **H3** — parts/oil/accessories;
- **HC3/CRM** — customer relationship, retention, leads/customer care;
- **Network Development** — channel, standards, coverage, people/process/premises;
- **Digital/system layer** — NMS, customer apps, MyHero dan tools terkait.

## Operating implication

Masalah H1 bisa punya root cause atau leverage dari domain lain: AHASS waiting room dapat menjadi sales prospect source; CRM memengaruhi repeat/retention; NOS People/Process memengaruhi execution; financing memengaruhi conversion.
