# Market Intelligence

## 🍼 10 detik

Jangan berhenti di angka sales. Market Intelligence mencari **kenapa** pasar bergerak dan apa implikasinya untuk target, activity, stock dan strategy.

## Research thinking from ASSDP

`identify problem → form hypothesis → test hypothesis → decide strategy`

Use existing data first, then field sensing/survey where needed.

## Evidence families to consider

- motorcycle market trend: Polreg/AISI/price;
- customer database: demographic/profile/payment/previous bike;
- macro/regional: BPS, BI, PDRB, inflation, commodity, employment/income;
- media/local events: disaster/weather/policy/event;
- competitor: product, price, promotion, activity, stock/availability;
- quantitative/qualitative customer research when the desk evidence is insufficient.

## Practical rule

A market explanation should connect an external/internal driver to a measurable business effect. “Cuaca”, “komoditas”, or “competitor promo” alone is not a root cause unless impact is evidenced.
