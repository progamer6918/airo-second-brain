# Demand Management

## 🍼 10 detik

Demand Management menghubungkan **market → forecast → demand/sales plan → supply/distribution → monitoring**, supaya supply tidak lepas dari demand nyata.

## System view from ASSDP

Training connects:

`Marketing Strategy → Demand Forecast → Demand Plan → Sales Plan → Supply Plan`

with market sensing, ATL/BTL, sales program, base demand/seasonality/growth, market size, product mix, distribution and stock monitoring.

## Top-down + bottom-up

AHM target/supply logic and Main Dealer bottom-up market plan must be reconciled. Key success is balancing supply and demand while maximizing market opportunity and controlling stock availability in potential areas.

## Forecast factors

- seasonality;
- trend/growth;
- new-model penetration;
- competition/share target;
- economic condition;
- weather/disaster;
- buying power;
- target/activity/promotion/stock controls.
