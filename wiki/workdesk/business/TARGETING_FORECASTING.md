# Targeting & Forecasting

## 🍼 10 detik

Target yang bagus bukan sekadar growth angka. Ia harus turun dari **potensi market → target share/sales → area/dealer → people → activity**.

## Target deployment logic

ASSDP Basic shows a sequence:

1. analyze market/economic driver;
2. set market by kabupaten;
3. set market-share expectation;
4. derive target retail sales by kabupaten;
5. assign target responsibility by dealer/coverage;
6. calculate people/POS/activity need;
7. break target to sales force.

Advanced wrap-up simplifies the sequence as historical trend → market potency → area mapping → sales target → #sales-force/productivity → activity.

## Forecast discipline

Forecast should combine recurring pattern with explainable and uncertain factors. It is not simply “same as last year” and should be continuously refreshed against market and actual achievement.
