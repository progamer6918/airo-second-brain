# Pricing, DP & Financing

## 🍼 10 detik

Credit competitiveness bukan cuma OTR. ASS perlu memahami DP, discount, installment, fincoy competitiveness dan portfolio risk karena semuanya memengaruhi conversion dan support leasing.

## Price intelligence

Historical ASSDP framework compares:

- OTR Honda vs head-to-head competitor;
- DP pricelist;
- average discount;
- actual/down-payment paid;
- installment;
- credit/interest-rate competitiveness.

The purpose is to make financing packages competitive and actionable in MD–Dealer–Fincoy discussion.

## DP composition

Track movement of DP layers by series and finance company; use it to read market condition and find where financing strategy has room to grow.

## FID / BA caution

Historical training treats FID/BA as risk indicators that influence financing-company appetite. Analyze trend by fincoy/series/dealer/DP layer/customer segment and do not maximize low-DP volume without considering portfolio quality.
