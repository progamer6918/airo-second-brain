# AT High

## 🍼 10 detik

AT High adalah business focus/competitive arena yang harus dibaca sebagai **market + product + dealer + customer + activity + competitor**, bukan hanya target tipe.

## Owner project evidence

Intermediate Owner project in Merangin framed AT High as a competitive market-share problem: total market grew while Honda share lagged Yamaha, with weak brand awareness and dealer focus skewed to faster-moving products.

The intervention combined:

- explicit target by month/dealer;
- brand-awareness activity;
- touring/roadshow/test ride;
- ATL/BTL;
- community/customer activation;
- monitoring sales, share, dealer/PIC execution and activity effectiveness.

## WorkDesk use

When AT High underperforms, inspect:

`market size/growth → Honda/Yamaha share → model/segment → dealer contribution → target user/area → stock/pricing/fincoy → people/productivity → activity frequency/effectiveness → awareness/consideration → funnel → corrective action`
