# Channel Management

## 🍼 10 detik

Channel Management mengelola **coverage dan kemampuan jaringan** agar penjualan serta pelayanan customer bisa berkembang.

## Core scope from ASSDP Basic 2023

- new dealer;
- replacement/coverage when dealer resigns;
- dealer upgrade;
- monitoring timeline/progress with Network Development;
- field supervision;
- POS standardization and productivity context.

## Operating logic

1. Plan next-year channel needs.
2. Identify coverage/performance gaps.
3. Coordinate new/upgrade/replacement with Netdev.
4. Monitor every milestone, not only diagnose delay after it happens.
5. If a dealer resigns, analyze root cause and how area coverage will be maintained.

## Caution

Specific 2023 dates/requirements (for example historical upgrade deadlines or POS class thresholds) are historical unless a current source reconfirms them.
