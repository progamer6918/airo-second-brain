# CRM & Leads

## 🍼 10 detik

CRM/leads mengubah customer interest menjadi proses yang bisa **assign → follow-up → prospect → SPK/deal → delivery → retention** dan bisa dimonitor.

## Intermediate CRM workshop evidence

The supplied workshop material shows multiple lead sources, validation, duplicate/repeat-order checks, assignment to dealer/FLP, multiple follow-up attempts/channels and lead-status progression.

## WorkDesk operating model

When a sales problem involves leads, inspect:

1. lead source/quality;
2. validation and duplicate/RO handling;
3. assignment speed/owner;
4. contact attempt/follow-up discipline;
5. prospect qualification;
6. SPK/approval/deal conversion;
7. delivery;
8. after-sales/retention/repeat opportunities.

## Link to NOS

Current NOS 2026 contains CRM and customer-follow-up responsibilities in H1/H23 process/people items, so CRM is part of network execution—not a separate marketing-only concept.
