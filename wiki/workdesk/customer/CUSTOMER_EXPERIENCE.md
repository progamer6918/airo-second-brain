# Customer Experience & Customer Centricity

## 🍼 10 detik

Customer experience bukan “ramah saja”. Ia adalah desain end-to-end supaya customer merasa mudah, cepat, jelas, dipercaya dan konsisten.

## External training lenses

### C.A.S.H.
Historical Advanced external material uses a shorthand: **Convenience, Action, Speed, Hassle Free**.

### NPS
Use Promoter/Passive/Detractor thinking to understand advocacy, neutrality and detractor risk. Exact scoring interpretation belongs to the training context; current company metric contract wins if different.

### Power of Moments
Four moment types are taught: **Elevation, Insight, Pride, Connection**—useful for deliberately designing memorable customer/employee experiences.

## Link to NOS

NOS turns customer-experience thinking into operational network requirements: premises, process, people, customer journey, follow-up, apps/loyalty and service consistency.
